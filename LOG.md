## 2026-10-30, session with Hidde: shares are counted now, and a published photo gets a share

Hidde asked what would make users bring others. The answer: measure first, then the moment people are proud. Before this, no share button counted anything. Google Analytics shows those visits as source "share". Each tap is logged as `share_tapped` (PostHog in the app, the events table on the web), and each visit arriving on a shared link as `share_arrived` (events table).
