# Barcelona, second deepening pass (2026-08-05) — the Montjuic walk

Goal set in the brief: open one clean new walk in a single neighbourhood rather than
scatter. Chosen: **Montjuic / Poble-sec**, because it is the only untouched cluster in
Barcelona that is (a) somewhere travellers already go, (b) dense enough to walk, and
(c) mostly free, which the two published Montjuic trees (bcn_005, bcn_006) are not,
they sit behind the botanical garden's paid gate.

Candidate screen: data/registers/barcelona-ail.json filtered to units == 1, ownership
not private, a real planting year, and inside the Poble-sec / Montjuic ring. Six survived
the screen. Cross-checked against OpenStreetMap, which carries the same municipal
catalogue as independently surveyed nodes.

## Access finding that applies to three of the six

The Jardins del Teatre Grec close to the public for the Grec festival, roughly
15 June to 7 August every year. Barcelona's own garden page carries "tancat" (closed)
in its URL slug for that reason. Every Teatre Grec entry must say this, because it
covers exactly the weeks a summer visitor would come.
