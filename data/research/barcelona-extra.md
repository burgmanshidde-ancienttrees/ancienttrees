# Barcelona, deepening pass (2026-08-05)

Goal: thicken the existing ten rather than scatter. Source of candidates:
`data/registers/barcelona-ail.json` (Ajuntament de Barcelona, Arbres d'interes local,
CC BY 4.0), filtered to single-unit, publicly accessible, dated entries lying close to
a pin we already publish.

Distance measured from each published tree. Three clusters carried the work:

- **Parc de la Ciutadella**, where we published only bcn_008 (the silk tree). Nine more
  register entries sit within 260 m of it. Three survived the bar.
- **El Raval**, where bcn_010 (the Misericordia palms) stood alone. One entry sits 70 m away.
- **Les Corts**, where bcn_007 (the Maternitat magnolia) stood alone. One entry sits 127 m away.
- **Park Guell**, where bcn_002 and bcn_003 stand 100 m apart. One entry sits 125 m away,
  and it carries a problem (see the olive, below).

Register cross-checks applied per CLAUDE.md: units, ownership, unit sanity, and a
coordinate check against OpenStreetMap, which carries the same catalogue as mapped nodes.

## VERIFIED 1 — Casuarina del parc de la Ciutadella (bcn_011)

- Casuarina cunninghamiana, register planting year 1894, catalogued 1994, 1 unit, Public.
- Register: girth 5.5 m, height 27 m, crown 20 m. bcnsostenible gives 22.5 m tall,
  perimeter 5 m at 1.3 m, crown 18 m. Two trunks fused at the base, splitting at about 90 cm.
- Second source: beteve.cat "Els arbres mes singulars del Parc de la Ciutadella"
  (independent broadcaster), which names it, gives the Australian origin, the
  second-largest-in-Barcelona perimeter claim, and the not-a-conifer point.
- Pin: register coordinate only. No OSM node for it. "Davant de la cascada del llac"
  puts it about 35 m from the Cascada monument. **location_precision: approximate.**
- Photo: Commons File:Casuarina del Parc de la Ciutadella3.JPG, CC BY-SA 3.0,
  Herodotptlomeu, Wiki Loves Earth Catalonia 2014. Viewed: full tree, daylight, colour,
  trunk and crown both readable, tree fills the frame, centre band is the tree. Approved.

## VERIFIED 2 — Roure blanc mexica de la Ciutadella (bcn_012)

- Quercus polymorpha, register 1887, catalogued 1997 (catalogue no. 0130-01-97), 1 unit, Public.
- Register: girth 2.5 m, height 8 m, crown 11 m. Low and leaning.
- Second source: beteve.cat, same article: the only specimen of the species alive in
  Catalonia, "viu a pocs metres de la cascada", and the leaves are not lobed.
- Pin problem: register says 41.388892, 2.186420; OSM node 6924278485 (same catalogue,
  address-geocoded) says 41.38829, 2.18680. 75 m apart. The Commons photo shows the tree
  standing beside the park's bandstand, which supports the OSM position, so that is the one
  used. **location_precision: approximate**, because two municipal renderings disagree.
- Photo: Commons File:Roure - Ciutadella 4.JPG, CC BY-SA 3.0, Herodotptlomeu, categorised
  under "Roure de Mexic del parc de la Ciutadella". Viewed: leaning trunk fills the centre
  of frame, daylight, colour, crown readable. Approved. (File 3 in the same series was
  rejected: the bandstand takes the centre band and the tree sits at the left edge.)

## VERIFIED 3 — Taronger de Louisiana de la Ciutadella (bcn_013)

- Maclura pomifera, register 1913, catalogued 1993 (0039-01-93), 1 unit.
- Register: girth 2.6 m, height 16 m, crown 19 m. bcnsostenible: 14 m tall, 2.30 m at 1.3 m,
  crown 13.5 m. Ranges used.
- Second source: beteve.cat "Un arbre desconegut i de fruit estrambotic", which describes
  the fruit ("de color verdos, gairebe florescent", channels "que poden recordar les d'un
  cervell"), says it is not edible, and that it falls in late autumn with some hanging on
  through winter. That fixes the seasonal peak.
- Pin: register 41.388569, 2.187622; OSM node 6924265087 at 41.38874, 2.187549 (19 m);
  iNaturalist observation 330848719 at 41.3887833, 2.1873783 (12 m). Three sources agree.
  **location_precision: confirmed.**
- best_time: months 10 and 11, kind fruit.
- Photo: the only open-licence image found in place is iNat 330848719, a close-up of a
  split fruit on the ground. Fails the Cadiz standard as a tree portrait. Not used.

## VERIFIED 4 — Eucaliptus vermell de la placa de les Caramelles (bcn_014), FLAGGED

- Eucalyptus camaldulensis, register 1904, catalogued 1994, 1 unit, Public.
- Register: girth 3.1 m, height 29 m, crown 13 m. guia.barcelona, the same municipal record
  rendered elsewhere: 26 m tall, 2.90 m perimeter. The city disagrees with itself.
- **No independent second source found** for the 1904 date. Everything traceable is a
  rendering of the same municipal catalogue. Published flagged, with the uncertainty stated
  in the story, per the rule that allows it.
- Access: stands inside the Casa de la Caritat grounds and is seen from Placa de les
  Caramelles, a public square open to the street. Casa de la Caritat is now the CCCB.
- Pin: register 41.382401, 2.167735; OSM node 6924165385 at 41.382393, 2.167705 (3 m).
  **location_precision: confirmed.**
- 70 m from bcn_010, so the Raval becomes a two-tree stop.

## VERIFIED 5 — Alzina dels jardins de Bacardi (bcn_015)

- Quercus ilex, register 1904, catalogued 1994, 1 unit, Public.
- Register: girth 2.1 m, height 16 m, crown 14 m. bcnsostenible: over 14 m tall, crown 8 m.
- Second source: Catalan Wikipedia "Jardins de Bacardi" and carrers.barcelona, which give
  the estate history independently of the tree catalogue: land known as Can Ermengol, bought
  by the Bacardi family late 19th century, house built for Baltasar de Bacardi i Janer,
  granddaughter Anna Bacardi i Ribas died 1958, heirs subdivided, the house was demolished
  for the widening of Travessera de les Corts, gardens expropriated by the city in 1963.
  Wikipedia also names the catalogued holm oak specifically.
- Renovation: the city approved a redesign of the gardens and surroundings in April 2026
  (metropoliabierta, barnadiario), 11,800 m2, sports areas, petanca, allotments. The oak is
  catalogued and therefore cannot be removed, but works may be under way. Said in access.
- Pin: register 41.380174, 2.125790; OSM node 6924087388 at 41.380142, 2.125771 (4 m).
  **location_precision: confirmed.**
- 127 m from bcn_007, so Les Corts becomes a two-tree stop.

## VERIFIED 6 — Ullastre del Park Guell (bcn_016), FLAGGED, MERGE DECISION NEEDED

- Olea europaea var. sylvestris (wild olive), register planting year **1771**, catalogued
  1996 (0117-06-96), 1 unit, Public.
- Register: girth 2.9 m, height 17 m, crown 21 m. bcnsostenible: 14 m tall, 1.6 m at 1.3 m,
  crown 13 m. Wide disagreement in the city's own records; ranges used.
- **The problem.** 1771 means 255 years. The published Barcelona page states flatly that the
  oldest tree in the city is the Gracia holm oak at about 250 years, and the question page
  is built on that answer. The difference between the two is not a measurement, it is a
  method: the Gracia oak was ring-dated by dendrochronologists, the olive carries the
  register's own approximate birth year with nothing behind it. That distinction is real and
  the story says it out loud, but the two claims still sit on the same site.
  **Do not merge this tree without deciding what the oldest-tree claim should say.**
- Pin: register 41.414909, 2.154906; OSM node 4618688164 at 41.41494, 2.15479; Commons photo
  EXIF GPS 41.414910, 2.154910. Three-way agreement. **location_precision: confirmed.**
- Photo: Commons File:Ullastre del Parc Guell P1500841.jpg, CC BY-SA 3.0, pere prlpz.
  Viewed: full olive crown over a Park Guell stone wall, daylight, colour, people for scale,
  centre band is the tree. Approved.
- Access: east sector of Park Guell, outside the ticketed Monumental Zone, same caveat as
  bcn_002 and bcn_003.

Full list with reasons is in `data/leads/barcelona.json`. Headlines:

- **Blocked as non-collectible ensembles:** Platans de les Rambles (273 units, the exact
  entry already killed once as bcn_008), Alzines de la placa Catalunya (44), Tarongers de la
  Casa de la Misericordia (33), Washingtonies de l'Avinguda Francesc Ferrer i Guardia (24),
  Arbreda de la placa del Nord (16), Xiprers calbs de la Ciutadella (7), Alzines del Parc del
  Laberint (8).
- **Blocked as private or access-restricted:** Livistones and Livistona of the Ateneu
  Barcelones (private club patio), Roure martinenc del parc Zoologic (inside the zoo
  enclosure), Teix de la Facultat de Geografia i Historia (interior faculty courtyard),
  Ginkgo and Teix dels jardins de la Universitat (register marks them private), Pi Josep de
  Vallcarca (private garden).
- **Leads, failed on evidence:** Robinia del parc de la Ciutadella (girth 4.3 m, but the
  register's age field reads "100 anys" rather than a year), Platan del parc de la
  Ciutadella (3.6 m girth, no date at all), Pitospor de la Ciutadella (no date, no height),
  Pins de fulla llarga del Himalaia (2 units, age "En estudi").
- **Leads, failed on the count or on age:** the two remaining monumental trees of the Sot de
  l'Estany (Fraxinus angustifolia and Acer pseudoplatanus), which would complete the set of
  four the city page already mentions, but would put a third and fourth entry in one ravine;
  Camforer del Jardi Botanic (1933); Lagunaria and Erythrina falcata of the Teatre Grec
  (1923); Alvocater de la placa de Can Bruixa (1945, 81 years).
