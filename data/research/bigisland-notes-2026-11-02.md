Banyan Drive facts used
- Planting: DeMille 20 Oct 1933, Babe Ruth 28 Oct 1933 (still stands in front of the Hilo Hawaiian Hotel per Keola), FDR 25 Jul 1934 (touched from his car), Earhart 1935. Wikipedia: 8 trees Oct 1933, 10 in 1934, 15 in 1935; about 50 remain. Source conflict: one travel summary put Ruth's tree at Grand Naniloa; Keola says Hilo Hawaiian.
- Threats: invasive gall wasp, one east-side banyan partially collapsed in 2022 (Big Island Now), plaques rotted or missing (Keola). The fatal July 2025 Kilauea Avenue banyan collapse is not on Banyan Drive.
- Book with every tree and honoree: Jane Hoff, The Trees of Banyan Drive. Friends of Banyan Drive (president Luana Richardson in 2022) would know positions.

Kona facts
- Queen Kapiolani brought four trees in 1882: two banyan cuttings from Iolani Palace (one to Hulihee Palace, one transplanted 1906 by the Maguire family to Kailua Pier), two Moreton Bay figs (Holualoa Bay one gone, Burgess one fell Oct 2025). Images of Old Hawaii /along-alii-drive.
- Register 1205 (Indian Banyan, 23 m girth, 19.64,-155.99, no locality) may be one of these banyans; not asserted.
