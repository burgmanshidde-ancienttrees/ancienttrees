# Cyprus, Limassol district pass (2026-11-02)

Verified (flagged): Laneia oak (CY-FD-16, confirmed pin via iNaturalist GPS 3 m plus All About Limassol map pin), Apesia terebinth (CY-FD-14, approximate).
Useful method: All About Limassol article pages carry a 'View on Map' coordinate in the HTML (grep for lat, lng); fetch with curl -A browser UA. iNaturalist API gives dated GPS observations with girth notes (photos mostly CC BY-NC, not usable).
Not found: any notable old tree inside Limassol town itself. Limnatis terebinth is a dead trunk (blocked).
Time-box hit before Kyperounta, Kissousa, Silikou, Potamiou, Sotira, Agios Therapon were researched.
The Department of Forests flyer (moa.gov.cy ... Four fold flyer.pdf) is a JPX scan with no text layer; needs OCR.
Laneia register conflict: register 6.10 m / 7 m versus 8.4 m girth / 26 m in the guide and iNat; likely different measurement, possibly the Royal Oak remnant.
