# DDG Champion Trees list, first look (2026-11-05)

Tip from Dorit Zimmermann (BUND Naturschutz München) by mail, 2026-11-05.

- Site: https://championtrees.ddg-web.de/ (the old championtrees.de does not resolve; ddg-web.de/championtrees.html redirects here). Deutsche Dendrologische Gesellschaft.
- Size: 12,981 trees, 395 collections, 214 genera, 2,130 national champions (Bundeschampions). Monthly updates, including trees that have gone ("abgegangene Einträge"), which makes it useful for life checks.
- Each record (e.g. /championtree-1368/, Erler Femeiche) holds: species, rank (DE and Land), Land, Landkreis, place, a location description, **access** ("Öffentlich zugänglich"), girth at 1.3 m, height, crown, estimated age, measurement history with who measured, condition, proper name, literature, and **GPS** (data-lat / data-lng on the page).
- Licence: "© Deutsche Dendrologische Gesellschaft", photos "© Bildrechte" per photographer. So it is a finding aid and a fact source, never an import: no photos, no text copied. Measurements and GPS read as facts, second source for existence and species as usual.
- robots.txt allows everything except /wp-admin/; sitemap at /wp-sitemap.xml.
- The NRW book Dorit mentions is probably Christoph Michels, "Starke Bäume in NRW" (Remagen, 2017), which the DDG records cite.
- Earlier use: single facts only (Munich Kaisereiche measurement via Wikipedia, a Stuttgart ranking, Bargischow). Never walked as a list. The DDG is also in the archive contacts file as a photo archive.
- Proposed first step: Munich (in data/index-kept.json), match the München Stadt and Landkreis records against our 63 trees (pins, girth, height), and record public-access records near the city as leads.
