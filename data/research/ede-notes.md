## 2026-10-27, the map's data, read the same day

- Open data, Public Domain Mark: data.overheid.nl dataset 6689, WFS layer Ede:monumentale-bomen, 11,106 municipal trees (3,885 Monumentaal). Kept in data/registers/ede-monumentale-bomen.json; verdict in OPEN_DATA_SURVEY.md.
- Of our 7 trees only the fire-station sequoia (ede_006) is in it: Monumentaal, > 75 cm, 15 to 18 m, planted 1885. Added as a source. The others are estate or forest trees, not the gemeente's.
- Leads: the densest old group is in Lunteren, Roskammersteeg (52.0829, 5.6206): six pedunculate oaks and a copper beech put at 1880, a beech lane put at 1900 just west. Classes only (> 75 cm), so they need a photograph and a reason beyond age before a page.
