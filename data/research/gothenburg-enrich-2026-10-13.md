# Gothenburg enrich pass, 2026-10-13

Found: Goteborgs Stad made the four boundary oaks (gransekar) naturminnen on 2025-09-23 (Stadsbyggnadsnamnden, par. 542 to 545). Each has its own decision document (Beslutshandling, Bilaga 1) with a 2024 girth measurement. Property to tree mapping (the decision texts give surroundings, not street names):

- Skar 751:81 (SBF-2025-00291): Skarsgatan lies west of the oak, villas on three sides, about 600 cm, 300 to 500 years est. = got_002 Skarseken. Strong match.
- Bo 750:797 (SBF-2025-00289): busy road one side, slope down to footpath, tram rails and Delsjobacken, about 620 cm, 300 to 500 years est. = got_003 (Delsjovagen). Strong match. Tree-campaign page tradplangoteborg.se/radda_granseken gives 602 cm at the narrowest point and "at least 500 years"; the 2011 city survey said 500 cm; city 2024 figure used.
- Torp 749:66 (SBF-2025-00292), on the Torp/Skar boundary, slope to a house, woodland on the other side, about 500 cm, "hundreds of years" = got_004 (Stora Torp / Skogshagestigen per vartgoteborg.se). Read from the March 2025 draft; final text not opened.
- Skar 751:93 (SBF-2025-00290): stairs just north leading up to broadleaf woodland, Japanese knotweed around it, about 400 to 500 cm, 300 to 500 years est., a little hollowness = by elimination got_005 (Laboratoriegatan). Weaker match; girth left out because it is a range and the mapping is by elimination.
