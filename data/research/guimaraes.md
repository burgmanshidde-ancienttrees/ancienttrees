# Guimaraes research notes (2026-08-06)

Register-first pass. Source: data/registers/portugal-icnf.json, filtered to concelho == "Guimaraes"
(21 entries total). Full concelho list checked (not just the 14 leads handed in the brief) to make
sure no closer, more genuinely public tree was missed.

## The 21 register rows for Guimaraes, by site

- Jardins do Paco dos Duques de Braganca (UF Oliveira, S. Paio e S. Sebastiao): 2x platano
  (Platanus hybrida Brot.), 150y @ 2011. SHIPPED as gui_001.
- Claustro da Igreja de N. Senhora da Oliveira / Museu Alberto Sampaio (same freguesia): 1x oliveira
  (Olea europaea), no age given. SHIPPED as gui_002, flagged (no confirmed age).
- Jardins Palacio Vila Flor, parterre inferior + superior (Urgezes): 8 + 10 cameleiras (Camellia
  japonica), 180y @ 2011, ~30m apart in the same garden. SHIPPED as gui_003 (one ensemble entry).
- Cemiterio da Atouguia / estacionamento (Creixomil): 3x cedro-do-himalaia (Cedrus deodara), one
  dated (100y @ 2011), two undated at an identical rounded coordinate (register rounding, not a
  literal single point; a later municipal candidatura added these two). SHIPPED as gui_004.
- Pousada de Santa Marinha da Costa (Costa): "Varias" conjunto arboreo, 6 species (Quercus robur,
  Quercus rubra, Castanea sativa, Cedrus atlantica, Cupressus lusitanica, Eucalyptus globulus),
  290y @ 2006, classified 1940 (DG 119). SHIPPED as gui_005, heavily flagged: the 290y figure cannot
  apply across the group (eucalyptus only reached Europe in the early 1800s per the pousada's own
  botanical guide, so a 290y eucalyptus is impossible). Anchored on the oak by the baroque water
  tank, which the pousada's own PDF roteiro botanico calls "some centuries old" without a number.
- Casa da Penha (Costa): 78 species, 790 specimens. NOT a collectible point, whole-forest inventory.
  BLOCKED.
- Casa da Ribeira (Ponte): pinheiro-de-weymouth, sequoia, cipreste-do-bucaco, cedro-do-himalaia,
  carvalho-alvarinho, faia, tulipeiro-da-virginia, liquidambar. 5.3km from the centre, well outside
  any cluster with the above. LEAD, unresearched, different cluster for a future pass.
- Casa de Margaride (Mesao Frio): cameleira, garden-conjunto (Camellia japonica, C. reticulata, Ilex
  aquifolium, Taxus baccata, Chamaecyparis lawsoniana, Cupressus lusitanica), pereira (Pyrus
  communis), diospireiro (Diospyros kaki), all 130y. Private aristocratic estate ("Villa Margaridi"),
  garden "can be visited" with the owner as guide (evasoes.pt, muralha.org); no standing public
  opening hours found. One of its camellias won Portuguese Tree of the Year 2024. NOT SHIPPED,
  access too uncertain to call "genuinely open to visitors" without a firmer source. LEAD, full
  writeup below, needs Hidde's call.
- Casa da Covilha (Fermentoes): 1x cameleira/japoneira, 300y @ 2018, the oldest single age in the
  whole Guimaraes set. Private historic house (Fernando Tavora architecture), guided visits
  "organized" by the family, no standing public hours found either. NOT SHIPPED. LEAD.
- Silvares (pinheiro-manso), Moreira de Conegos (pinheiro-manso), Citania de Briteiros
  (pinheiro-manso): all 4km+ from the centre, unresearched this pass. LEADS, out of cluster range.
- Serra da Penha / Monte da Penha (Costa): pseudotsuga, eucalipto, and a "diverso arvoredo" of firs
  (abeto-do-pacifico, da-siria, do-rei-boris, turco, de-fraser, pinheiro-da-macedonia). Part of the
  Casa da Penha 790-specimen arboretum above, 2.2-2.7km out, same non-collectible-point problem.
  LEADS/BLOCKED, not researched individually this pass.

## Verification sources used

- cm-guimaraes.pt's own "Arvores e Arvoredo de Interesse Publico" page (master list, confirms
  classification numbers and dates for every site above).
- labpaisagem.pt (Laboratorio da Paisagem, the municipal landscape lab) individual tree pages for
  the planes and the cedars.
- Museu Alberto Sampaio's own site for the cloister/olive tree and its ticket price.
- cm-guimaraes.pt's camellia-garden and Centro Cultural Vila Flor sources for the Palacio Vila Flor
  garden's opening hours and free access.
- Pestana/Pousadas de Portugal's own "Roteiro Botanico" PDF for Santa Marinha da Costa (12 signed
  species, garden history, the "recovered as a leisure place for guests and visitors" line that is
  the basis for treating it as open rather than hotel-guests-only).
- Wikipedia PT/EN, DN/Lusa and local outlets (maisguimaraes.pt, creativenews.pt, ominho.pt,
  guimaraesdigital.pt) for Casa de Margaride and Casa da Covilha history and access status.

## Judgment calls flagged for Hidde

1. Casa de Margaride and Casa da Covilha: both have real, register-classified, well-documented
   trees, but both are private houses with occasional owner-guided visits rather than standing
   public hours. Left as leads rather than shipped. If Hidde considers "guided tours available,
   book ahead" close enough to Blarney/Fota's paid-entry model, both are ready to write from the
   material already gathered.
2. Pousada de Santa Marinha da Costa: shipped, but on a judgment call. It is a hotel, but the
   grounds are register-classified since 1940 and the property's own visitor material describes the
   park as restored "for guests and visitors" (not guests only). Flagged for a second look.
3. The register's 290y age for the Santa Marinha conjunto cannot be literal across all six species
   named (eucalyptus is a 19th-century introduction to Portugal). Shipped with a wide, honestly
   uncertain age range rather than repeating the register figure.
4. The cloister olive tree's connection to the 1342 miracle legend is the city's founding story, but
   there is no source tying this specific trunk to that date. Shipped as the legend plus an honest
   "age unconfirmed" rather than implying a 680-year-old tree.
