# HKU Digital Arboretum (Gavin Coates), Hong Kong finding aid

Found 2026-10-20 through Gavin (WhatsApp, via Sally Bunker). Site: https://www.digarb.hku.hk
The map's data is a public GeoJSON: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/digarb/DA_MapDB/refs/heads/main/MapDB%20-%2020260511.geojson
(1,182 YouTube teaching videos, each with species, place, month and a coordinate where it was filmed; 324 species.)

**Licence: none.** The GitHub repo has no licence; the videos are his. So: a finding aid only.
Coordinates and species are facts we may check against; no video, thumbnail or text goes on the site,
and it never counts as a register. It does count as one independent source for "this tree stands here"
(a botanist filmed it on that date at that spot).

What it is and is not: a map of where Gavin teaches (his words: "the distribution of Gavin"),
mostly common species and street plantings, not a survey of old trees. Only 15 videos sit within 25 m
of an Old and Valuable Tree of the same genus (12 distinct OVTs).

Uses:
1. Pin checks. It caught hkg_003 (Kam Tin Tree House) about 300 m off the register point it cites;
   fixed 2026-10-20.
2. Life checks: a dated video at an OVT is evidence the tree stood on that date.
3. Leads: "Kuk Po, A Ficus microcarpa Tree House Conservation" (2025-02-27, 22.528556, 114.238654)
   and the Mui Wo OVT banyan (LCSD IS/2) are not on our page.
4. best_time hints: the filming month for flowering trees (Bombax ceiba in March, Delonix regia),
   never a calendar by itself.
