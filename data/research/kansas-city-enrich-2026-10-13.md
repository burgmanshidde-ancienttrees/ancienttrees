# Kansas City enrich pass, 2026-10-13

Register: all four trees have the owner's own accession page at lindahall.org (fetched, HTTP 200) and are listed on the arboretum Champion Trees page as Greater Kansas City Champion Trees (16 listed). Accessions: Shumard Oak 34-21-1 (planted 1968), Double Flowered Horsechestnut 2-4-1 (cultivar Baumanii), European Beech 14-2-1 (cultivar Riversii, planted 1971, so it is our Rivers Purple Beech), Hardy Rubber Tree 13b-1-1 (planted 1975). The champion list is kept by Heartland Tree Alliance (Bridging the Gap), formerly Powell Gardens; the formula is points from girth at 4.5 ft, height and spread.

Measurements: none. The library pages and the champion page publish no girth, height or spread numbers. Left open.

Pin: open. The accession pages only embed a map image with no coordinates in the page source. Overpass (overpass-api.de, one retry on kumi.systems) timed out, so OSM tree nodes were not checked. The grounds are a walk-through arboretum, so a location_site would be too large; not offered.

Season (beech): not set. No evidence found that this copper beech shows a striking moment worth a calendar entry.

Planting years (1968, 1971, 1975) are specimen records from the owner but give no age claim beyond young trees.
