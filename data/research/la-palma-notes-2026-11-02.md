# La Palma verify pass, 2026-11-02

Official catalogue: none found. No Cabildo de La Palma or Gobierno de Canarias catalogue of singular trees with coordinates turned up (WebSearch, three Spanish queries). Only mentions: a 2005 onwards Gobierno de Canarias inventory and a 2009 publication "Arboles y arboledas singulares de Canarias" (decree stalled), neither fetched. Wikidata has "Pino del Consuelo" tagged "arbol singular en Fuencaliente" (Q132783195), the only inventory trace. GRAFCAN/IDECanarias layers not tried (time).

What worked: Commons geosearch plus Commons file metadata API for licence and date; Nominatim search for OSM natural=tree nodes (Overpass returned 502/406); OSM API node tags for each node; iNaturalist API (observations within 100 m, licences per photo); Wikidata SPARQL box query; lifecheck.py.
Dead ends: komoot (finding aid, coordinates only), Atlas Obscura 403, Medusa wiki (service suspended), visitlapalma trail pages (no tree detail), vegueta ULPGC article (too many redirects), search for chestnuts, laurels, Los Tilos named trees.
Photos: Pino de la Virgen (Commons CC BY-SA 4.0) and Dragos Gemelos (CC BY-SA 3.0) usable; Pino del Consuelo iNat photos are CC BY-NC (not usable). lpa_004 photo from iNat CC BY.
Not checked: Cumbre Vieja eruption damage; none of the delivered trees lie in the 2021 lava zone (El Paso is east of the Cumbre Nueva ridge, Breña Alta and Fuencaliente-Consuelo outside the flow area, Garafia far north).
Island yields little: 3 solid candidates, one weak (lpa_004). Floor of 4 reached only with the flagged drago.
