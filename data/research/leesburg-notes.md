# Leesburg / Loudoun verification notes (2026-11-02)

Naming body: Loudoun County Big Tree Registry (county forester; 32 trees, measured 2025). Coordinates and a `Public_` flag per tree sit in the public ArcGIS layer
https://services1.arcgis.com/MxjRokvPm7bjslyR/arcgis/rest/services/Big_Tree_with_photos/FeatureServer/0 (query?where=1=1&outFields=*&f=json).
Caution: three rows (Silver Linden, Shellbark Hickory, one Ginkgo) have shifted columns in that layer; the PDF list (loudoun.gov DocumentCenter/View/216553) is correct.
`Public_` = -1 means the county counts it visible/accessible from public property; it is not proof of access.

Second layer: Town of Leesburg Tree Walk (leaflet via Wayback of leesburgva.gov showpublisheddocument/27422): 23 downtown trees, mostly private yards; only the Heritage Tree (Ida Lee) and cemetery trees are public.
Oatlands has a GIS map of 17 historic trees (hosted.arborprousa.com/DNGN-Oatlands, 403): a later pass could ask Oatlands for it.

Delivered 8: four at Saint John the Apostle (a compact site, all within 110 m) plus Morven Park, Oatlands, Ida Lee, and one Sterling (Algonkian) tree. Leesburg page floor (4) is met by Saint John's alone; the others are within 12 minutes by car of downtown. All pins approximate (county coordinates). No photographs looked for.
Hosts: www.leesburgva.gov 403 live (Wayback works); atlasobscura 403; loudountimes 429 and paywalled; Overpass not tried (OSM API used).
Open risks: Oatlands ginkgo not confirmed alive after the July 2026 hornbeam removal; Ida Lee oak is one town sentence plus a pin at the farmhouse.
