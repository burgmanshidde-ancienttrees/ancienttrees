## Shared context: Jardim Teófilo Braga (Jardim da Parada)

- The garden holds FOUR trees classified "de interesse público": 1 taxodium, 2 metrosideros, 1 Sequoia sempervirens.
  Confirmed independently of ICNF by:
  - Wikipedia PT, "Jardim Teófilo Braga (Lisboa)": https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jardim_Te%C3%B3filo_Braga_(Lisboa)
    lists Sequoia sempervirens, Taxodium distichum and two Metrosideros excelsa as the four classified trees.
  - Junta de Freguesia de Campo de Ourique, património page (search snippet; page 403s to fetch):
    https://www.jf-campodeourique.pt/patrimony/19/jardim_da_parada
    "quatro árvores classificadas de Interesse Público: dois Metrosídero, um Taxodium e uma Sequoia".
- NOTE A SPECIES CONFLICT: ICNF records the taxodium as *Taxodium mucronatum* (Montezuma cypress).
  Wikipedia PT and the Junta both say *Taxodium distichum* (bald cypress). Unresolved; see tree 1.
- Popular name "Jardim da Parada": the block was the parade ground (parada) of the Campo de Ourique barracks
  before it became a garden. Official name honours Teófilo Braga, 2nd President of the Portuguese Republic.
- Garden features: coreto (bandstand), central lake with ducks/fish/turtles, kiosk, playground,
  a reading box built into an old telephone booth. Area 0.39 ha, fills one entire city block.
  Restructured in the early 21st century (Wikipedia PT).

### Reconciliation finding: three classified, not four

The ICNF register holds exactly THREE processos for Jardim Teófilo Braga (AIP11065946I taxodium,
AIP11065947I + AIP11065948I metrosideros), all classified by the same
**Despacho (extrato) n.º 8497/2018, Diário da República 2.ª série n.º 169, 3 September 2018**.
The Sequoia sempervirens that Wikipedia PT and the Junta both count as the fourth "classified" tree
does NOT appear in the national register.

Independent confirmation that the real number is three: the Metro de Lisboa environmental impact study,
quoted by Polígrafo, says *"poderá haver impactos indiretos sobre as três árvores classificadas
presentes no Jardim Teófilo Braga"*, and Metro's own press release commits to
*"uma zona de proteção de 20 metros de raio"* around *"3 exemplares arbóreos classificados (fitomonumentos)"*.
- https://poligrafo.sapo.pt/fact-check/e-verdade-obras-de-nova-estacao-de-metro-em-lisboa-vao-obrigar-a-abater-arvores-no-jardim-da-parada
- https://www.metrolisboa.pt/institucional/2022/10/04/metro-de-lisboa-ira-repor-o-jardim-da-parada/

Exactly the register-versus-municipality mismatch CLAUDE.md warns about. Never write "four classified trees".

### The Metro threat, 2022 onward (relevant to all three garden trees)

Lisbon's Metro is building a Campo de Ourique station whose worksite takes about 15% of the garden.
A residents' movement, "Salvar o Jardim da Parada" (salvarojardimdaparada.pt), fought it from 2022.
What Metro committed to publicly: a 20 m protection radius around the three classified trees with no
intervention inside it plus ongoing phytosanitary monitoring; six unclassified *Celtis australis* (lódãos)
felled for the site, four of them replanted in the same spot afterwards and two elsewhere in the garden;
50 new trees planted in the parish as compensation.
- https://www.dn.pt/local/arvores-do-jardim-da-parada-serao-preservadas-garante-metro-de-lisboa-15202352.html
- https://amensagem.pt/2022/09/29/campo-de-ourique-obras-metro-lisboa-arvores-jardim-da-parada-superilhas-crescer-dobro-aumentar/
- https://lisboaparapessoas.pt/2022/08/03/salvar-o-jardim-da-parada-movimento-metro-campo-de-ourique/

The 2024 protest tagging system, quoted verbatim from the Commons description of "Fitas de aviso"
(a photo of the movement's own sign):
  "Fita preta – árvore a abater / Fita vermelha – árvore em risco elevado de morrer / Fita amarela – árvore em risco"
So residents colour-coded the garden's trees black/red/yellow. Metro's public position is that the trees
to be felled are six unclassified Celtis australis, not the three classified ones. Do not write that a
classified tree is condemned; the evidence does not support it.
