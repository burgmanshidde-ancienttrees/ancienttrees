## The garden itself: Jardim 9 de Abril (a.k.a. Jardim das Albertas / Jardim da Rocha do Conde de Obidos)

STATUS: verified, and one important correction to how the site should describe it.

**It is NOT the museum garden.** Two different gardens sit side by side on Rua das Janelas Verdes and
sources conflate them constantly:
- *Jardim do Museu Nacional de Arte Antiga* — the museum's own terraced garden with the cafe/esplanada,
  entered through the museum building at Rua das Janelas Verdes 17. Free (no ticket needed) but bound to
  museum hours, and the museum is under renovation (Time Out says reopening in the second half of 2026).
- *Jardim 9 de Abril* — a municipal public garden immediately adjacent, on the Rocha do Conde de Obidos,
  with the miradouro over the Tagus. Wikipedia PT: "jardim de acesso publico permanente". This is the
  garden the ICNF register names as the `lugar` for all three trees.

Facts established:
- Public, free, permanent access; not dependent on any museum ticket or museum opening hours.
  Source: https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jardim_9_de_Abril ("jardim de acesso publico permanente").
- Two entrances: the stairway up from Avenida 24 de Julho, and Rua das Janelas Verdes.
  Source: https://www.portaldojardim.com/pdj/2009/09/25/jardim-9-de-abril-lisboa/
- Area 3,402 m2, of which 2,373 m2 green space (portaldojardim, same page; it still lists the pre-2012
  parish "Prazeres", the current parish is Estrela).
- Named for 9 April 1918, the Battle of La Lys, where the Portuguese Expeditionary Corps was overrun.
  Two older names survive in use: Jardim das Albertas, after the Carmelite Convento das Albertas whose
  cerca (walled garden) this was, and Jardim da Rocha do Conde de Obidos, after the rock it stands on.
  Source: https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jardim_9_de_Abril
- Contains the Miradouro da Rocha do Conde de Obidos, a memorial to Jose Antonio Marques (founder of the
  Portuguese Red Cross, whose headquarters is the neighbouring palace) and a plaque to Teixeira de Pascoaes.
- Hours: no published closing hours found for the municipal garden; treat as open access, do not invent hours.
- **Independent confirmation that all three ICNF trees are here**, in one sentence:
  "Tipuana tipu, Brachychiton e Phoenix dactylifera - arvores classificadas de interesse publico"
  https://www.portaldojardim.com/pdj/2009/09/25/jardim-9-de-abril-lisboa/
  (also lists Punica granatum, Jacaranda mimosifolia, Viburnum tinus in the same garden)
