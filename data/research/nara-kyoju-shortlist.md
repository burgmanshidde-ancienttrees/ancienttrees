Nara city, 巨樹・巨木林データベース, PDL 1.0). All girths and heights are the
2000 survey figures, so every tree has grown since. The register carries no

## The register's measurements for trees we already publish

Applied to data/cities/nara.json 2026-09-08: nra_001 (800 cm, 30 m),
nra_002 (720 cm, 22 m, the register names it 春日大社の大杉), nra_006 (342 cm,
18 m), nra_008 (425 cm, 15 m).

**Not applied, and it is the biggest tree in the whole Nara extract.** A
kusunoki of **1110 cm** girth and 28 m at 春日野町飛火野, which is Tobihino,
where our nra_005 stands. Not applied because nra_005 is an ENSEMBLE, "The
Camphors of Tobihino", and hanging a single-trunk measurement on an ensemble is
the bridge claim this project keeps catching itself making. It also disagrees
with our page: the register's age band reads 99年以下 against our "about 140
years", which is implausible at 11 metres round and suggests a multi-stem
measured as one. A verify pass should settle which trunk carries the 1110 cm
and whether nra_005 should be split into a named single tree.

## What is NOT here

The veteran cedar Hidde photographed above the Nigatsu-do steps
(34.68900/135.84381 approx) is in no ministry record. Nara city has 58 sugi over
300 cm in the survey and none of them is at 東大寺. Either the 1988/2000 survey
missed it or it measures under the 300 cm floor.
