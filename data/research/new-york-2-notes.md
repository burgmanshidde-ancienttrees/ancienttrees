# New York 2 verify pass (Manhattan and Bronx), 2026-11-02/03

Method that worked: NYC Parks Great Trees pages (nycgovparks.org) are bot-blocked to curl, WebFetch and Wayback (202 empty or 429). Names of Great Trees came from fetched 6sqft, Village View, West Side Rag and Fort Tryon Park Trust pages plus search-result titles. Pins and alive checks came from the NYC Parks Forestry Tree Points dataset on NYC Open Data (hn5i-inap; tree-level points, dbh, condition, updated dates into 2026, query with within_box/within_circle and tpstructure='Full'). A Great Tree whose Parks diameter equals a unique forestry point in the named park is treated as that tree (nyc_031 to 033, 036, 037). The 1985 Bronx list (Wave Hill, Woodlawn, NYBG, Pelham Bay, Corlear Ave sycamore) was read from a Wikipedia user draft that transcribes Parks entries; not an authority, so those stay leads without coordinates.
Hosts: nycgovparks.org/prd/php1/www2 mirrors blocked; archive.org 429; lizchristygarden.us certificate expired; overpass not tried (OSM tree nodes in Marcus Garvey Park had no species tags).
