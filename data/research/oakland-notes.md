## Leads
- Santa Cruz Cypress, Regional Parks Botanic Garden, Tilden (37.8935,-122.2426): Cal Big Trees state champion (finding aid only); Wikimedia Commons has a Cupressus abramsiana photo at RPBG (2016) but no independent source for the individual champion tree; no iNaturalist hit within 2 km. Garden is free. Lead: no photo or exact pin in reach.
- Oracle Oak (Quercus x morehus), Contra Costa (37.93197,-122.13378): Cal Big Trees national champion lead; web search found nothing independent, location unknown (private? Lafayette/Briones area). Lead.
- Hollyleaf Cherry (37.68084,-122.07853): Hayward area, champion; not checked, outside Oakland/Berkeley clusters.
- Manzanita Recreation Center oak, East Oakland: SF Chronicle column speculates it may be Oakland's oldest coast live oak (friend's estimate about 400 years); no records, no coordinate. Lead.
- Lake Merritt Lakeside Park coast live oaks: Community for Lake Merritt tree guide (communityforlakemerritt.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/lake-merritt-tree-guide.pdf) is an image-only PDF, could not read it; a Conservancy post reports losing one of the largest oaks near the Edoff Bandstand. Lead for a session that can read the PDF.
- Mills College Bell Tower Oak (beside El Campanil, Julia Morgan): old postcard only; campus now Northeastern, access unclear. Lead.
- Mountain View Cemetery: Olmsted design, public, Pattillo's "Trees of Mountain View Cemetery" booklet lists 28 trees (pgadesign.com PDF URL returned an HTML error page). No individual tree with age or position found in fetchable sources. Lead: read the booklet.
- Mather Redwood Grove (UC Botanical Garden, 5 acres) and Joaquin Miller Park Big Trees trail: groves, no individual tree sources found.
- Berkeley campus: 'Tall Tree Tales of Cal' audio tour has 16 trees (only 3 named on the news page: London planes on Campanile Esplanade, southern magnolia north of Sproul Hall, eucalyptus grove); City of Berkeley landmarked six campus trees or groves in 1996 (not named on fetched pages). Campus olive moved from Giannini Hall site; red cedar at West Circle planted 1930s (Berkeleyan 1995). Leads. "Trees of the Berkeley Campus" 1976 PDF at creeks.berkeley.edu/node/55 not read.
- London Plane row, Campanile Esplanade: avenue, would be blocked as an ensemble; kept as lead.
