## phl_004 life check (weeping hemlock at the Meadowbrook gate)
Verdict: alive, flagged thin. The Morris Arboretum blog 'Legacy weeping hemlock' (https://www.morrisarboretum.org/blog/legacy-weeping-hemlock, 18 April 2024) says the snowstorm of 13 February 2024 brought down a legacy weeping hemlock (Tsuga canadensis f. pendula) near Morris Pond (another Morris piece says Swan Pond), and that its sister tree, moved in 1947 from the foot of the Germantown slope to the Meadowbrook Avenue gate (root ball about five tons), is still growing happily. No later dated observation. Correction for the writer: the felled twin stood at the pond, not at the gate; the gate tree is the survivor. The Great Trees page also calls the weeping Canada hemlock exceptionally large and mature.
