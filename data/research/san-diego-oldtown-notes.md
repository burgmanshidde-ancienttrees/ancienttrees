## Leads
- Sixth Avenue and Laurel Street Moreton Bay fig, Balboa Park edge (~32.72541,-117.15932, iNat 76762994 GPS 10 m, another obs 2026-07-17 nearby via lifecheck RECENT): Reader 1991 calls it one of three mature local Ficus macrophylla. Balboa Park, one source, no age; not delivered per scope.
- Old Town big pepper tree beside the old Caltrans building, Taylor/Juan St (SOHO 2016, enews/0716trees.htm: "very large and very old"): no age, planter or coordinate; Caltrans moved to 4050 Taylor St in 2006, building site unclear.
- Juan Street Old Town pepper trees (SOHO: 18+ proposed for removal, "very old" per Bowen): an avenue, no dated specimen.
- Presidio Park peppers "oldest near the Serra cross" (SOHO Presidio Plant Survey, sohosandiego.org/presidio/schinusmolle.htm): several trees, no age, no coordinate. Full survey by Parish Rye (Presidio Park Horticulture Facebook page) not reachable; would name specimens. Jerabek plant tour (sandiegohistory.org 1962/april/plant, 1969/july) 403 to WebFetch; try via browser pane.
- Whaley House yard peppers: SOHO quiz answer key says the Whaley children planted them; not confirmed standing, private museum grounds with ticket.
- Mission San Diego de Alcala: olives said planted 1769 but garden olives dated 1860 by one low-quality source; no named specimen. NOTE: San Diego Magazine's Peyri pepper tree (planted 1830, 190 years on) is Mission San Luis Rey in Oceanside, not San Diego. Do not attribute it here.
- Kensington library park Canary Island pines (four, 1913 photos, SOHO 2009): same site as sdg_013; could be a recognise note rather than separate point.
- Kensington parkway pepper trees (30 of 37 originals, c.1910, Conserve-a-Tree application 2018, SOHO 2024): an avenue, not designated, litigation; blocked as an avenue but note for the writer.
