# Santa Barbara verify pass notes (2026-11-03)

Naming bodies: City of Santa Barbara list of designated city landmarks (santabarbaraca.gov, PDF) includes trees; the city's historic/specimen tree list (Director's list of official trees under the municipal code) is not online. Santa Barbara Beautiful "Tree of the Month" and Independent "S.B. Big Trees" columns are useful pointers.

Delivered: sbr_001 fig (confirmed), sbr_002 City Hall pepper tree (flagged), sbr_003 library gums (flagged), sbr_004 Alameda Park dragon tree (flagged), sbr_005 Lotusland dragon tree (ticketed, one source). Downtown cluster: fig, pepper, library gums, Alameda Park all inside about 1.5 km. 1 of 5 behind a ticket.

## Leads
- Doremus Stone Pines, E Anapamu St 300-800 blocks, 79 trees, city landmark designated 10/7/1997 (1908 and 1929 plantings); avenue, individually designated per Independent. Possible collectible if one flagship trunk is picked; many replaced 2024-26 (KEYT 2026-03-10: 40 new stone pines).
- Fernald Eucalyptus Tree, east side of 400 block of Santa Barbara St, city landmark 10/7/1997, c.1890 (saved by Pearl Chase per edhat.com ?p=74103); species said lemon-scented gum; no coordinate or recent life check found.
- Sugar Gum champion, park in front of Old Mission (next to Rose Garden), Eucalyptus cladocalyx, finding aid only (smgrowers.com list); iNat 2025-04-05 at 34.43805,-119.71290 (14 m) is nearby. Needs an independent source.
- Old Mission Sycamore stump, NE side of Mission (about 34.438,-119.713), alive and resprouting but cut back; not a tree to visit (yankeebarbareno.com, 2026-04-02).
- Santa Barbara Botanic Garden champion Island Oak (Quercus tomentella), paid; archives.sbbotanicgarden.org/node/36326 photo "Island Oak - Champion Tree" 2018-12-31; no age, location in garden or independent size source found.
- Historic Flame Tree (Brachychiton x roseus), Parking Lot 12, State and Gutierrez; weak and diseased in 2013, status unknown (independent.com 2013/10/10).
- Mount Calvary Monastery dragon tree (front yard, E Los Olivos St, north of Mission) and the Mission parking dragon tree: iNat obs near 34.4375,-119.7131 (2025-09-13, 9 m). Sbbeautiful names both among California's largest; which tree is which not settled, monastery is an institution. Possible view-only street tree under the 2026-11-04 ruling if the tree is clearly seen from the street; needs a map check.
- Franceschi Park Harpephyllum caffrum champion (finding aid only).

## Blocked
- Lotusland Monterey cypress on the Great Lawn, c.130 years: felled May 2017 (goodnewsnetwork.org, 2017-04-30).
- Cottage Hospital Moreton Bay Fig, 320 W Pueblo St, city landmark 2011: hospital grounds, no evidence of public access.
- Dragon tree at private estate Montecito and Casa del Herrero: private.
- Tree of Light, 100 W Carrillo St, city landmark 1977: not shown to be a tree.
- Goleta, Carpinteria, Stow House trees (Goleta Sycamore, Ward Holm Torrey Pine, Spanish Fir etc.): outside Santa Barbara city; separate places.
- Courthouse sunken garden: no named old tree found; 1929 plantings only, one cedar already felled.
