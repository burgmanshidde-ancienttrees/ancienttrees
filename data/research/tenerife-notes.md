# Tenerife verification pass notes (2026-11-02)

## Cabildo catalogue licence

Verdict: the Cabildo's island catalogue exists and is usable as an open source under CC0, but only by way of the Cabildo's site-wide legal notice; it is not a downloadable dataset and it carries no coordinates table.

- What it is: the "catalogo interactivo de flora singular" / arboles monumentales (25 regional, 105 insular, 168 municipal protection entries, announced as 323 specimens in the press; the two figures do not add up and the catalogue is described as dynamic, with additions and removals). Public and private ground, natural, urban and agricultural.
- Where it lives now: the old host arbolesmonumentales.tenerife.es now 301-redirects to https://www.tenerife.es/medioambiente (the old pages /index.php/list-especies/details/9/<n> are dead). The records are now individual Liferay pages at https://www.tenerife.es/w/<slug> (e.g. /w/drago-de-san-antonio, /w/pino-de-buen-paso, /w/pino-canario-pino-del-senor), indexed from https://www.tenerife.es/listado-de-especies (JavaScript paged, only 20 rows reachable without rendering). They are fetchable with curl or urllib using an ordinary browser User-Agent. Each record has: municipality, location text, altitude, height (m), perimeter (cm), protection level (regional, insular, municipal), land type (public or private), observations, history, and a "Como llegar" Google Maps short link. Zero in the height or perimeter field means "not recorded".
- Coordinates: yes, but only through the Maps short link on each record (curl -I the maps.app.goo.gl link and read the 3d/4d values). Where the Cabildo dropped a real pin the coordinate has 7 decimals and was within 1 to 10 m of OSM nodes and geotagged Commons photos for three trees checked (Drago de San Antonio, Drago del Cristo, Pino de Buen Paso). Where the coordinate is rounded (28.33,-16.39 for Pino del Senor; 28.2,-16.53 for Pino Derrengado) it is only town-level: treat as approximate or ignore.
- Open data portal: datos.tenerife.es (CKAN) has no tree or monumental-tree dataset (package_list searched for arbol, singular, monument, flora, arboleda: none). The portal itself is described on datos.gob.es as CC BY, which does not apply to a tree dataset because none exists there.
- Licence proving sentence (Cabildo de Tenerife aviso legal, https://www.tenerife.es/portalcabtfe/es/aviso-legal): "permite la reutilización de los contenidos y de los datos para todo el mundo y sin limitaciones temporales ni restricciones, en los términos establecidos por el instrumento legal CC0 de Creative Commons". The same page asks to cite the source and not to alter or distort the information, and says a work's own licence prevails over the notice, and forbids use of the Cabildo's logos and marks. Caveat for the dispatcher: this is the portal-wide notice, not a notice printed on the tree records themselves; the Liferay record pages show only "Aviso legal / Propiedad intelectual" footer links.
- Use here: finding aid and fact source (heights, perimeters, protection, land type, coordinates through the Maps link) with source URL recorded per tree; no text copied. Bulk import not attempted: the list is not reachable without rendering and a bulk read of 300 pages was out of scope for this pass.
- Data quality warnings: records are old (some textual notes date from the 1990s: the Drago del Cristo ficha says the crown split on 28 May 1991 and suggests transplanting it); the ficha does not record vitality; many entries are PRIVATE land (about two thirds of the dragos checked: not shippable).
- Spanish Wikipedia's municipal articles for Arafo, Tacoronte and Los Realejos (2026 edits, citing the Cabildo pages) repeat and extend the catalogue and flag recently lost trees (the Drago de San Francisco, Realejo Bajo, gone in March 2026).

## Method notes

- OSM Overpass answered a bbox query for natural=tree with a name (needs a User-Agent; plain curl got 406 and later two requests timed out). The named-tree list is in the leads file.
- Wikidata around-query for tree items within 50 km: only the four live trees and Q135580408.
- web search (WebSearch summary) is weak for obscure Tenerife trees: most useful result was a search that surfaced the catalogue's own slugs.
- Hosts: candelaria.es skipped (as told); tenerife.es handshakes time out intermittently, retry works; museosdetenerife.org PDF took over 60 s to download and could not be read (no PDF tool); tenerifeon.es returns 403 to WebFetch; web.archive.org blocked by the fetch tool.
- Not done: Jardin Victoria, the Plaza del Charco laurels, Garachico, Seminario and Adelantado plaza trees in La Laguna, the Liceo de Taoro drago, because no source with size or age was found in the time box; they remain open.
