# us-photo-batch-1: trees that cannot ship (2026-10-22)

- King Arthur Tree (Garfield Grove, Three Rivers CA): DEAD. Wikipedia: "died in the Castle Fire of 2020" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Arthur_Tree). Never ships; the Commons photo is of the living tree (2013).
- Seven Sisters Oak (Mandeville LA): PRIVATE. Stands in the front yard of a private residence in Lewisburg, Mandeville; Atlas Obscura says the owner asks visitors to get permission before entering the property (https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/seven-sisters-oak-louisiana, seen via search summary only) and no public-access arrangement was found. Hard rule 10: permission needed, not a home-free view. Could return as a lead if a public viewing point from the street is found. Wikipedia: girth 39.6 ft in 2016, largest registered live oak (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seven_Sisters_Oak).
- Duffie Oak (Mobile AL): ACCESS NOT SETTLED. Wikipedia gives only coordinates 30.68608,-88.06202; a single user-submitted iNaturalist note places it at 1129 Caroline Avenue, a residential address, and no source says who owns the ground or whether it can be seen from a public street. Alive: Wikipedia lists a Sept 2023 photograph. Facts for a later lead: southern live oak, about 300 years, circumference 30 ft 11 in, height 48 ft, spread 126 ft; Alabama Forestry Commission famous and historic tree 2003 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duffie_Oak).
- Council Oak Tree (Hollywood Seminole Reservation, FL): ACCESS NOT SETTLED. On a tribal reservation at US-441 and Stirling Rd, NRHP-listed 2012 with the NPS record address marked restricted (npgallery.nps.gov NRIS 12000992, search summary); in use for tribal inaugurations through 2015+. No source says a casual visitor may walk up, and tribal permission may be needed. Alive: yes by the Seminole Tribune reports (undated in my notes). Pin: Wikidata 26.045732,-80.209656, Commons geotag 26.04543,-80.209547 (33 m apart). Lead only until a source says the site is open to visitors.
- Fairmount Park Montezuma Bald Cypress Trees (Riverside CA): PIN CANNOT BE STATED. City of Riverside Landmark no. 61 (only source: Wikipedia list of landmarks), plural "trees" with no count, no species or measurements, and the Wikidata pin (33.996086,-117.376351) sits 700 m from the Commons photo geotag (33.993075,-117.382833), both inside Fairmount Park. Not one collectible point. Photo is CC0 (Josiah.r.white, 27 July 2026). Revisit if a park map shows where the cypresses stand.
