# Vietnam notes (verify pass 2026-11-02, Hanoi and Hoi An)

## National register: Cay Di san Viet Nam (Vietnam Heritage Trees)
- Run by VACNE (Hoi Bao ve thien nhien va Moi truong Viet Nam, vacne.org.vn) through its Vietnam Heritage Tree Council. First recognition 1 Oct 2010: nine muom (Mangifera foetida) at Voi Phuc temple, Hanoi. Press counts vary: 182 trees (older Dan Tri), 611 (Hanoi Times, Nov 2024), "over 7,000" (VietnamPlus, 2025 anniversary).
- Criteria in press: wild tree over 200 years (older rule 200 to 300), planted tree over 100 years, or special scientific, historical, cultural value; from 1 Jan 2022 one report adds minimums (25 m height, 6 m girth for timber trees).
- No downloadable list found. Records exist only as per-recognition news items on vacne.org.vn (English pages /<slug>/e<id>.html) and press reports. Ages are estimates, usually "about" or "over"; girth and height rarely given.
- Reuse terms: NOT found. No licence statement was seen on the fetched VACNE pages. Treat as facts-only use, with the recognition cited per tree; a data import is not cleared. Needs a licence read or a request to VACNE before any register layer.
- Heritage law does not cover the title: it is an association's honour (Bao Chinh phu, Aug 2024), so it carries no legal protection and no vitality field. Eight of the nine first-recognised trees at Voi Phuc died within four years.

## Hanoi finding
- The recognised trees are mostly outside the centre (Thanh Oai, Son Tay, Dong Anh, My Duc, Ba Vi, Phu Xuyen, Ung Hoa). Inside the city proper only Voi Phuc (muom) turned up with a certificate. Temple of Literature, Hoan Kiem lake, Quan Thanh, Botanical Garden: no recognition found for any individual tree. Hoan Kiem lake trees described as "unofficial heritage" (VietNamNet 21 Sep 2023).
- Two verified (flagged), below the floor of four. Photo: nothing sought.

## Hoi An finding
- Hoi An keeps its own list: Decision 2627/QD-UBND of 14 Nov 2014, 46 protected old trees. Individual list not found online; only Da Ke (Da Ken) tree and Yem Thuy Dao banyan have heritage-centre pages (hoianheritage.net, Hoi An Heritage Conservation Management Centre).
- VACNE recognised four tree groups on Cu Lao Cham (April 2015); the island is a boat trip, crossing time not verified.
- Hoi An is now in Da Nang city (OSM address after the 2025 merger); keep Hoi An as the place name.
- Three verified (flagged), below the floor of four.
- OSM has one named banyan node in the old town (Tran Cao Van, node 11581238672).
