# Published tree walks / tree-by-tree lists — German-speaking and Central Europe
Scouting sweep, 2026-08-05. URLs only, no text or images copied.

## Berlin
- Route exists: WEAK as a *walk*, GOLD as a *list*
- URL(s):
  - Wikipedia per-district Naturdenkmal lists, e.g. https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liste_der_Naturdenkmale_im_Bezirk_Mitte (sister lists exist for the other 11 Bezirke)
  - Senate overview: https://www.berlin.de/sen/uvk/natur-und-gruen/naturschutz/schutzgebiete/naturdenkmale/
  - Bezirk Mitte official: https://www.berlin.de/ba-mitte/politik-und-verwaltung/aemter/umwelt-und-naturschutzamt/naturschutz/artikel.246730.php
  - Charlottenburg-Wilmersdorf official search (37 ND trees): https://www.berlin.de/ba-charlottenburg-wilmersdorf/verwaltung/aemter/umwelt-und-naturschutz/naturschutz/baeume/
  - Baumlehrpfad Lietzenseepark (~14 trees, interactive map): https://www.berlin.de/ba-charlottenburg-wilmersdorf/verwaltung/aemter/umwelt-und-naturschutz/naturschutz/naturschutzprojekte/artikel.112912.php
  - Baumlehrpfad Böcklerpark, Kreuzberg (trees to ~200 yrs): https://www.berlin.de/ba-friedrichshain-kreuzberg/aktuelles/bezirksticker/2023/baumlehrpfad-am-boecklerpark-eroeffnet-1359794.php
  - Open data / geodata: https://daten.berlin.de/ (Baumbestand WFS/WMS; ND layer sits in FIS-Broker "Schutzgebiete und Schutzobjekte nach Naturschutzrecht")
  - NGO context page: https://www.bund-berlin.de/themen/stadtnatur/baumschutz/naturdenkmal/
- Publisher: city (Senate + each Bezirk's Umwelt- und Naturschutzamt); Wikipedia mirrors the Verordnung; BUND Berlin as NGO commentary
- Coverage: GOLD kind. 708 natural monuments citywide, 638 of them trees (rest are boulders), fixed by the Naturdenkmal-Verordnung. Verified by fetching the Mitte list: 132 objects in Mitte alone (126 trees), each row carrying common name, scientific name, street address, GPS coordinates via geohack, protection purpose and often a photo. Charlottenburg-Wilmersdorf holds 37. What the lists do NOT carry: age, girth, height. So species and position are solved, age is not.
- Walkable: spread across the whole city. Naturdenkmale are scattered over 12 Bezirke, many in private gardens, cemeteries and clinic grounds. The two Baumlehrpfade (Lietzensee ~14 trees, Böcklerpark) are genuine one-afternoon loops but are species-teaching paths, not ancient-tree routes, and Lietzensee's signage is under renovation with some trees already removed.
- Official designation list rather than a tourist article? YES, the Naturdenkmal-Verordnung is the legal register; the Wikipedia lists are derived from it.
- Licence: Berlin open data publishes under Datenlizenz Deutschland by-2.0 (attribution "Geoportal Berlin / [dataset]") or dl-de/zero-2.0 depending on dataset; verified on the Baumbestand WFS/WMS entries, NOT separately verified for the Naturdenkmale layer, so read that one before importing. Wikipedia tables are CC BY-SA.
- Judgement: the single richest German source found. Species and pin are free; age is the whole research cost, and Berlin's ND designation is about beauty/rarity rather than age, so many entries will be handsome but not ancient. Expect 20-40 citywide candidates worth a second source, and cluster-pick a Bezirk (Mitte, Pankow) rather than treating Berlin as one walk.

## Vienna
- Route exists: GOLD as a list, WEAK as a walk
- URL(s):
  - Official Naturdenkmal register, browsable per district: https://www.wien.gv.at/umweltschutz/naturschutz/gebiet/naturdenkmaeler/ (e.g. .../bezirk-09.html, .../bezirk-21.html)
  - Overview: https://www.wien.gv.at/umwelt/naturdenkmaeler
  - Open data, point layer: https://www.data.gv.at/katalog/dataset/383408d4-89b8-42df-af53-5c89f91e0cc7 ("Naturdenkmäler Standorte Wien")
  - Wikipedia mirror: https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liste_der_Naturdenkm%C3%A4ler_in_Wien
  - Baumkataster (all 230,000 public trees, with planting year, girth, crown, height): https://www.wien.gv.at/umweltgut/public/ and https://www.data.gv.at/datasets/c91a4635-8b7d-43fe-9b27-d95dec8392a7
  - Historic MA22 register PDF: https://www.zobodat.at/pdf/MA22-Wien_86_0001-0015.pdf
  - Agenda-21 Baumspaziergang Freihausviertel, 59 trees: https://la21.wien/app/uploads/2024/10/Baumspaziergang-2022-Liste-der-Baeume.pdf and https://la21.wien/projekt/stadtbaeume/
  - Third-party map of the Baumkataster: https://treesinvienna.eu/de/ , https://baumkarte.at/
- Publisher: city (MA 22 Umweltschutz for Naturdenkmale, MA 42 Stadtgärten for the Baumkataster); Agenda 21 Wien (city-funded neighbourhood NGO) for the Baumspaziergang
- Coverage: GOLD kind for the register. 434 Naturdenkmäler citywide, individual trees and tree groups being by far the commonest type. Verified district 9: 11 entries, each with street address, scientific species name and ND number (450 to 859), one carrying a 3D model link. What is missing everywhere: age and dimensions. But Vienna is the one city where that gap is fillable from the same publisher, because the Baumkataster carries planting year, trunk girth, crown diameter and height per tree.
  The Freihausviertel Baumspaziergang PDF is a genuine tree-by-tree walk: 59 trees, each with tree number, street, scientific and German species name, planting year, girth in cm, crown diameter and height. Verified by opening it. Caveat: it is a *street tree* walk in the 4th district, planting years 1980 to 2018, so almost nothing in it is old. Useful as a template of what Vienna publishes, not as a source of ancient trees.
- Walkable: the ND register is spread across all 23 districts; the Freihausviertel walk is one compact afternoon.
- Official designation list rather than a tourist article? YES for the wien.gv.at Naturdenkmal pages and the data.gv.at layer.
- Licence: City of Vienna open data is CC BY 4.0 (Namensnennung). Applies to the Naturdenkmäler point layer and the Baumkataster. This is the cleanest licence position of any city in this sweep.
- Judgement: cheapest research pass of the six. Register gives designation, species and address; Baumkataster gives girth and planting year for the same tree, which is close to a second source from an independent city department. Expect 15-30 Vienna trees to survive a two-source bar, with good clustering potential in the Innere Stadt, Prater and Schönbrunn/Hietzing.

## Hamburg
- Route exists: WEAK
- URL(s):
  - Official Naturdenkmale page: https://www.hamburg.de/politik-und-verwaltung/behoerden/bukea/themen/naturschutz/naturdenkmale-52292
  - Wikipedia list: https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liste_der_Naturdenkmale_in_Hamburg
  - Schutzgebiete WFS/WMS metadata: https://metaver.de/trefferanzeige?docuuid=785BC91D-81B8-458B-859B-8936AC4C753A
  - Straßenbäume online (street tree cadastre, map): https://www.hamburg.de/politik-und-verwaltung/behoerden/bukea/themen/hamburgs-gruen/baeume/strassenbaeume-online/strassenbaeume-online-karte-274310
  - Ohlsdorf cemetery dendrological walk essay: https://www.fof-ohlsdorf.de/kulturgeschichte/2000/71s27_spaziergang.htm
  - Ohlsdorf nature trail N7 (1.5 km, 23 boards): http://www.laurustico.de/index.php/hh-gruen/617-naturpfad-ohlsdorf
  - Stadtpark Baumlehrpfad + PDF plan: https://stadtparkverein.de/projekte/baumlehrpfad/ , https://stadtparkverein.de/wp-content/uploads/2013/07/baumlehrpfad.pdf
  - Loki-Schmidt-Garten (botanical garden, Uni Hamburg): https://www.botanischer-garten.uni-hamburg.de/
- Publisher: city (BUKEA) for the register and the cadastre; Förderkreis Ohlsdorfer Friedhof (NGO journal) for the dendrological walk; Stadtpark Verein (NGO) for the Baumlehrpfad
- Coverage: the official register is a DEAD END for our purpose. Verified: Hamburg has only 11 Naturdenkmale in total, and exactly ONE of them is a tree, the Eibe am Neuländer Deich (designated 1936, image caption calls it 1000 years old). The other ten are moors, brackish waterways, ditches, a clay pit and a garden. Hamburg protects trees through the Baumschutzverordnung generally rather than by naming individuals, so there is no citywide list of named veteran trees.
  Best actual substitute is Ohlsdorf: the world's largest garden cemetery, 389 ha, 36,000 trees of which more than 2,000 are over 100 years old. The fof-ohlsdorf essay names roughly 15 to 20 individual specimens with species and a cemetery grid reference (S 7, R 32, AC 14) and occasional dimensions. That is a real, checkable list, but it is a thematic essay rather than a sequential route, and it is from 2000, so vitality needs checking per tree.
  Stadtpark Baumlehrpfad has a PDF plan but is explicitly NEW plantings since 2008, worthless for ancient trees. Straßenbäume online carries genus, species, planting year, girth and crown diameter per street tree, useful for dating a specific tree; the page states no licence, so licence must be verified before any import.
- Walkable: Ohlsdorf is one large afternoon on foot but the cemetery itself is 389 ha, so a cluster means picking one section (Linneteil, Alter Teil, Millionenhügel). The single ND yew is out in Neuland, far south.
- Official designation list rather than a tourist article? The 11-entry register is official but nearly empty of trees. The Ohlsdorf source is an NGO essay, not a designation list.
- Licence: not stated on Straßenbäume online; Hamburg Schutzgebiete data sits in the MetaVer/Transparenzportal, licence per record, unverified here. The Ohlsdorf essay is copyrighted text, cite only.
- Judgement: no cheap register route. Hamburg would be a normal, expensive research pass built around Ohlsdorf plus a handful of famous individuals; realistically 4 to 6 trees survive a two-source bar without a lot of work, and the grid references in the Ohlsdorf essay would need on-the-ground or Commons corroboration for pins. Rank Hamburg below Berlin, Vienna and Munich.

## Munich
- Route exists: GOLD as a list, NO as a published walk
- URL(s):
  - Wikipedia list, organised by Stadtbezirk: https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liste_der_Naturdenkm%C3%A4ler_in_M%C3%BCnchen
  - Interactive Naturdenkmal map: https://stadtgeschichte-muenchen.de/naturdenkmal/naturdenkmal_karte.php
  - City tree protection campaign: https://stadt.muenchen.de/infos/baumschutzkampagne.html
  - Surrounding district list: https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liste_der_Naturdenkm%C3%A4ler_im_Landkreis_M%C3%BCnchen
- Publisher: city of Munich (the Verordnung of 21 July 2021 is the legal instrument); Wikipedia and stadtgeschichte-muenchen.de render it
- Coverage: GOLD kind. 117 Naturdenkmäler covering 200 individual trees, per the 2021 ordinance. Verified the Wikipedia table: organised by district, each row giving German and Latin species name, street address, GPS coordinates in DMS, ownership status (city / state / PRIVATE) and cadastral parcel, protection rationale, and a photo for many entries with Commons category links. Age and dimensions are not columns, though individual entries carry them in prose (the Röth-Linde on Nederlingerstraße at the Westfriedhof is given as 300 years and 23 m).
- Walkable: spread across all districts, but 200 trees in a compact city means real clusters are likely (Altstadt-Lehel, Maxvorstadt, Nymphenburg/Neuhausen, the Englischer Garten edge). Run scripts/cluster_register.py on it once imported.
- Official designation list rather than a tourist article? YES, the 2021 Verordnung.
- Licence: the Wikipedia tables are CC BY-SA; the underlying ordinance is German official work. No open-data endpoint found in this sweep, so the practical import path is the Wikipedia table plus the ordinance, not an API.
- Judgement: the best German city in this sweep after Berlin, and better clustered. 200 named trees with species, address and coordinates makes research selection rather than search. WATCH the ownership column: a meaningful share are on private parcels, which hard rule 10 excludes, so filter on ownership before shortlisting. Expect 25-40 candidates to clear a two-source bar, and Munich probably ships a full ten more easily than Hamburg ships four.

## Prague
- Route exists: YES, the best find of the sweep. A published tree-by-tree walking route AND an official register.
- URL(s):
  - Walking routes hub: https://www.prazskestromy.cz/vylety-pesky/
  - City-centre route, 3.7 km, Karlovo náměstí to Hellichova: https://www.prazskestromy.cz/vylety-pesky/2-za-stromy-v-centru-prahy/
  - Route: Za stromy přírodního parku Říčanka, 5.5 km, Dubeč to Uhříněves (same hub)
  - Route: Ze Satalic do Vinoře, 5.8 km (same hub)
  - Audio trail, ~8 km, 6 stops, QR codes at the trees (Cholupice / Točná / Komořany, Praha 12): https://www.prazskestromy.cz/vylety/naucna-audiostezka/
  - What a památný strom is: https://www.prazskestromy.cz/stromy/pamatne-stromy/co-jsou-pamatne-stromy/
  - Official national register ÚSOP (AOPK ČR): https://drusop.aopk.gov.cz/portal/ and https://aopk.gov.cz/pamatne-stromy
  - City environment portal: https://portalzp.praha.eu/pamatnestromy
  - Wikipedia list: https://cs.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seznam_pam%C3%A1tn%C3%BDch_strom%C5%AF_v_Praze
  - District mirror example: https://www.praha12.cz/prazske-stromy/d-49304
- Publisher: prazskestromy.cz is by dendrologist Ing. Aleš Rudl with Agentura Koniklec o.p.s. (NGO, Swiss Contribution funded), carried on official city and district sites, so semi-official. ÚSOP is the national register kept by AOPK ČR (state agency). portalzp.praha.eu is the city.
- Coverage: GOLD kind on both layers. 196 trees hold památný strom protection inside Prague as of January 2025 (about 25,000 nationally). Verified the Wikipedia table: columns are name, image, district/location, species (Czech, e.g. "dub letní"), TRUNK GIRTH IN CM, Wikidata id, Commons gallery link and remarks, organised by district, with coordinates per tree. Age is not a systematic column, which matches every other register in this sweep.
  Verified the walking routes page: three routes, each with distance, start and end point, mapy.cz map link, three on-site information boards, and individually named trees with species and precise position (memorial ash, boundary oak, oak alley, planes, lindens, poplar, alder). The centre route is the directly usable one at 3.7 km.
- Walkable: YES, genuinely. The centre route is one afternoon on foot, 3.7 km. The other three are 5.5 to 8 km and sit on the city's edge (Dubeč-Uhříněves, Satalice-Vinoř, Praha 12), reachable but not central.
- Official designation list rather than a tourist article? BOTH exist: ÚSOP is the legal register, prazskestromy.cz is the curated walk on top of it. That combination is exactly the Setubal pattern that made that pass cheap.
- Licence: prazskestromy.cz footer says "© 2016, Pražské stromy, Všechna práva vyhrazena" (all rights reserved), so it is a SOURCE TO CITE, never to copy text or photos from. Czech Wikipedia tables are CC BY-SA. ÚSOP/AOPK licence not verified in this sweep, check before importing the register layer.
- Judgement: cheapest and best-clustered city of the six. The centre route hands us a ready-made walk with the second source built in, and ÚSOP verifies species and girth independently. Expect a comfortable 10 trees to clear a two-source bar, and Prague could ship as two clusters (centre + one outer route) rather than one thin list.

  - Local protected areas overview: https://budapest.imami.hu/helyi-hirek-kirandul-csalad/21-termeszetvedelmi-terulet-budapesten

- Coverage: middle kind, leaning gold. BP Fatár holds 236,000+ trees, and inside that a curated set of 100 NEVEZETES FA (notable trees) each with a short write-up, plus 38 locally protected areas and 300+ ha of city forest. That curated 100 is the closest Budapest equivalent to a register of remarkable trees, and it is exactly the right size. What I could NOT verify in this sweep: the web map at infogardenweb.hu is JavaScript-driven and returned no readable content to a plain fetch, so whether the 100 are browsable and exportable from a browser, and what fields each carries, is unconfirmed. Assume a run will have to drive it in a real browser.
  The Turista Magazin feature is a proper secondary source for 6 individually named trees with species, place and age: Sophora japonica at Buda Castle (140-220 yrs), Morus nigra on the Tabán slope (200-250), Tilia cordata at Pesthidegkút (250-500, believed Budapest's oldest), Cedrus libani on Kondor út in district II (~130 yrs, girth over 2 m), Platanus at Margaret Island (200 yrs), plus Normafa old-growth. It is an article, not a route.
- Walkable: no route published, so clustering must be derived. Margaret Island and Buda Castle/Tabán are two obvious compact clusters; Pesthidegkút and Normafa are hill suburbs, both a real trip out.
- Official designation list rather than a tourist article? Partly. BP Fatár is the city's own cadastre and the 100 notable trees are its own curation, so yes; the walk-shaped content is all magazine journalism.
- Licence: none stated anywhere I could reach. BP Fatár is free to use, which is not the same as openly licensed. Treat as unlicensed until proven otherwise; cite, do not copy, and do not take its photos.
- Judgement: no route to ride, so a Budapest pass costs more than Prague or Vienna, but the 100-tree curated set means candidate discovery is nearly free once someone opens the map properly. Realistically 8 to 12 trees clear a two-source bar (BP Fatár plus Hungarian press/hiker coverage), enough for a full page. Rank it fourth, behind Prague, Vienna and Munich, ahead of Berlin on cost-per-tree and well ahead of Hamburg.

## Cross-city notes for whoever runs these
- NO German or Austrian register in this sweep carries AGE or GIRTH as a column. Species, address and coordinates come free; age is always the research cost. The two exceptions worth knowing: Vienna's Baumkataster (planting year + girth per tree, CC BY 4.0) and Czech ÚSOP / cs-wiki (girth in cm per tree).
- German Naturdenkmal designation rewards beauty, rarity and Eigenart, not only age. A designated tree is not necessarily an old tree. Filter on species and girth, not on the designation alone.
- Munich's Wikipedia table has an OWNERSHIP column that flags private parcels. Filter on it before shortlisting; hard rule 10 excludes those.
- The Wikipedia "Liste der Naturdenkmale in [X]" pattern is consistent across German cities and is the fastest way to see a city's register without hunting a Verordnung PDF. Worth trying first for the unreached German cities above.
