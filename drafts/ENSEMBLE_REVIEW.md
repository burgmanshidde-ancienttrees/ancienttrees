# The 104 trees we refused for not being one tree

Hidde asked to go through these himself (2026-08-13: "this one is more difficult - allow me to look at all of those 1 by 1").

The rule they failed says an entry must be one identifiable tree, or an ensemble only when the ensemble itself is the destination: compact, famous for being exactly that, with one obvious place to stand. The Lomanstraat tunnel passes, a kilometre of planes on La Rambla does not. We already publish good ones under it (Bastion Vught's three trees, the Sint Agathaplein chestnuts), so the question per entry is only: is there one place to stand where the thing you came for is in front of you?

My suggestion is in italics and is a guess from the note alone, not a verdict. Coordinates open in Google Maps where the lead recorded them.
