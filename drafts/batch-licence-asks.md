# Licence batch, rewritten 2026-08-16 in Hidde's own style

Every register this project is blocked on, as one batch, ready to go out
together. Rewritten from the Arnhem mail he supplied as the calibration sample:
**name and what he builds in the first line, the goal stated plainly, one
concrete ask, one personal tree question, done.** The old drafts ran 250 to 350
words each and explained themselves three times; these run 120 to 160.

What is deliberately NOT in them: endpoint names, field lists, entry counts,
and our own publishing rules. He is asking for a licence, not demonstrating
research, and the corpus rule is that public copy never narrates our method.

What stays in every one, because it is what keeps him out of trouble later: the
site is free today and there may be a paid tier, said in one sentence and never
softened into "non-commercial".

## 1. Woodland Trust, Ancient Tree Inventory

To: ancienttreeinventory@woodlandtrust.org.uk
Subject: Written permission to use Ancient Tree Inventory data

Hello,

My name is Hidde and I am building ancienttrees.app, a platform for remarkable
trees. My goal is to get people excited about old trees and to encourage them to
go outside and look at them.

Your licence asks for written permission before Ancient Tree Inventory data
appears on a public mapping site, so I am asking. I would like to show a
selection of ATI trees in UK cities, starting with London, credited as your
licence specifies and linked back to the Inventory. We would only publish trees
that are publicly accessible, and we always honour a location you withhold.

The site is free and has no ads, but I am building an app with a paid tier
later, so I will not claim non-commercial. I am open to whatever terms work for
the Trust.

And either way: which ancient tree do you think more people should go and see?

Kind regards,
Hidde
ancienttrees.app

## 3. City of Sydney, Register of Significant Trees

To: Council@cityofsydney.nsw.gov.au
Subject: Licence for the Register of Significant Trees

Hello,

My name is Hidde and I am building ancienttrees.app, a platform for remarkable
trees. My goal is to get people excited about old trees and to encourage them to
go outside and look at them.

Your Register of Significant Trees is the best tree data I have found anywhere,
and I would like to use it to build a Sydney page, credited to the City with a
link back. We would only publish trees your own data marks as publicly
accessible or visible from the street.

Your street tree data carries a CC BY 4.0 licence but the significant trees
layer has none, so I am asking rather than assuming. The site is free and has no
ads, but I am building an app with a paid tier later, so I will not claim
non-commercial.

Would you licence it, CC BY or whatever you prefer?

And either way: which tree in Sydney do you think visitors should see?

Kind regards,
Hidde
ancienttrees.app
