# FOR HIDDE: cold mail to the Jardim Botanico da Universidade de Coimbra

Paulo's suggestion in his mail of 2026-09-02: he knows nobody who can supply
photographs of Coimbra's monumental trees, and says to write to the people at
the botanical garden.

**What is true before you send it.** Coimbra is live with four trees and not one
photograph, the only Portuguese city on the site in that state. Of the four,
exactly ONE stands in their garden: cbr_001, the bunya pine at the Arcos do
Jardim, 6.3 metres round and classified in 2010. The other three are not theirs
to answer for (two in the Mata Nacional de Vale de Canas, which is ICNF ground,
and the ESAC camphor at Bencanta), so the mail asks about their own garden and
nothing else. Asking a botanical garden for photographs of a national forest is
how a first mail gets ignored.

**The address:** jardim.botanico@uc.pt, +351 239 855 215. Their own contacts
page refuses machine fetches (403 on every attempt), so this comes from the
search index of that page plus a second listing that agrees. Worth one glance
before sending; a wrong address costs the whole approach.

**Paulo is named as the referrer.** He is a public blogger who told us to write
to them, so this is the warm-intro convention rather than passing his mail
around. Cut the line if you would rather not, the mail stands without it.

**The second address, not drafted yet and probably the likelier reply:** ESAC,
gac@esac.pt. Their camphor (cbr_004) is the tree they themselves put forward for
Arvore do Ano 2026, announced on their own site in December, so somebody there
has already written about it and photographed it. How that contest ended I have
not checked, and the mail should not guess. Say the word and I write it.
