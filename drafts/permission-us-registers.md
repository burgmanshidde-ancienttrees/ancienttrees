# FOR HIDDE: three permission asks that unlock the American cities

Written 2026-08-15, after the North America scouting pass. Your own strategy
call that morning: "there is huge potential in canadian and american semi big
cities like boston." The scouting found the data exists and is excellent. It
also found that the three best sources are blocked on licence rather than on
absence, and all three are the kind of body that says yes to a small
non-commercial-ish project when asked politely.

What each one is worth, so you can judge whether the minutes are worth it:

| Source | Blocks | Would unlock |
|---|---|---|
| Texas A&M Forest Service, Big Tree Registry | "personal, educational, and other non-commercial purposes" | **Austin** (8 in Travis County), and the best-shaped data of the whole pass: live ArcGIS layer, per-tree coordinates, a public/private flag and a condition field |
| Live Oak Society (Louisiana Garden Club Federation) | "must get written permission... for any purpose whatsoever" | **New Orleans** (139 entries in one sampled file), **Charleston** (34), **Savannah** (5). Thousands of named, measured live oaks across the South |
| American Forests, National Champion Trees | no licence at all, plain all-rights-reserved footer | the national champion list as a verification source; locations are county-level so it is a lead source, not a map layer |

Three things are true of all three drafts below and are deliberate: each says
plainly what we are (a free site, with an app and a paid tier intended later,
because saying "non-commercial" would be a lie we would have to walk back),
each names exactly which data we want rather than asking for everything, and
each offers the credit and the link, which is the only thing we have to trade.

Send them from your own address, one at a time. If Texas says yes, Austin
opens the same week; if the Live Oak Society says yes, three southern cities
open at once and that is the single biggest American unlock available to us.

---

## 1. Texas A&M Forest Service (Big Tree Registry)

Contact: the Big Tree Registry coordinator via texasforestinfo.tamu.edu, or
the Forest Service's general information address.

Subject: Permission to map the Texas Big Tree Registry on ancienttrees.app

Hi,

I run ancienttrees.app, a free site that maps remarkable old trees city by
city, with a story, an exact location and sources for each one. I am Dutch and
I am working through the world's tree registers one country at a time.

Your Big Tree Registry is the best-organised state list I have found anywhere,
and I would like to use it for Austin: the coordinates, species and
measurements for the Travis County trees, credited to Texas A&M Forest Service
with a link back to the registry on every page.

Your site says the content is for personal, educational and non-commercial
use. I should be straight with you: the site is free today and there are no
ads, but I am building an app and there will be a paid tier eventually, so I
do not want to claim non-commercial and then change it.

Would you be willing to give permission on those terms? Happy to agree
anything you need about attribution, or to leave out private-land trees
entirely.

Hidde
ancienttrees.app

## 2. The Live Oak Society (Louisiana Garden Club Federation)

Contact: the Live Oak Society chairman, via the LGCF contact page.

Subject: Permission to map Live Oak Society trees in New Orleans

Hi,

I run ancienttrees.app, a free site that maps remarkable old trees city by
city: one page per city, each tree with its story, its exact spot and the
sources it rests on.

I have been reading the Live Oak Society register and it is extraordinary. No
register in Europe names its trees the way yours does. I would like to put the
New Orleans ones on the map, and later Charleston and Savannah: name, girth and
location, credited to the Live Oak Society with a link on every page.

Your register says written permission is needed for any use, which is why I am
writing rather than assuming. To be straight about what we are: the site is
free with no ads today, and there will be an app with a paid tier later, so I
would rather ask properly than call it non-commercial.

If there are trees you would rather we left off, say which and we will leave
them off. We already do that for trees whose owners want them private.

And whatever you decide about the data: if you know a live oak in New Orleans
that visitors should see and most lists miss, I would love to hear about it.

Hidde
ancienttrees.app

## 3. American Forests (National Champion Trees)

Contact: American Forests, general or programme address.

Subject: Using the National Champion Trees register as a source

Hi,

I run ancienttrees.app, a free site mapping remarkable old trees city by city
with sources for every claim.

I would like to use the National Champion Trees register as a verification
source: when we publish a tree that is a state or national champion, we would
say so and link to your register. We are not looking to republish the list
itself, and your locations are county-level anyway, so this is about citing
you rather than copying you.

There is no licence or terms page on the site, only the copyright footer,
which is why I am asking rather than assuming. The site is free with no ads;
an app with a paid tier is planned, so I would rather be clear now.

Would that be all right with you?

Hidde
ancienttrees.app
