## 1. Texas A&M Forest Service (Big Tree Registry)

Contact: the Big Tree Registry coordinator via texasforestinfo.tamu.edu, or
the Forest Service's general information address.

Subject: Permission to map the Texas Big Tree Registry on ancienttrees.app

Hi,

I run ancienttrees.app, a free site that maps remarkable old trees city by
city, with a story, an exact location and sources for each one. I am Dutch and
I am working through the world's tree registers one country at a time.

Your Big Tree Registry is the best-organised state list I have found anywhere,
and I would like to use it for Austin: the coordinates, species and
measurements for the Travis County trees, credited to Texas A&M Forest Service
with a link back to the registry on every page.

Your site says the content is for personal, educational and non-commercial
use. I should be straight with you: the site is free today and there are no
ads, but I am building an app and there will be a paid tier eventually, so I
do not want to claim non-commercial and then change it.

Would you be willing to give permission on those terms? Happy to agree
anything you need about attribution, or to leave out private-land trees
entirely.

Hidde
ancienttrees.app

## 2. The Live Oak Society (Louisiana Garden Club Federation)

Contact: the Live Oak Society chairman, via the LGCF contact page.

Subject: Permission to map Live Oak Society trees in New Orleans

Hi,

I run ancienttrees.app, a free site that maps remarkable old trees city by
city: one page per city, each tree with its story, its exact spot and the
sources it rests on.

I have been reading the Live Oak Society register and it is extraordinary. No
register in Europe names its trees the way yours does. I would like to put the
New Orleans ones on the map, and later Charleston and Savannah: name, girth and
location, credited to the Live Oak Society with a link on every page.

Your register says written permission is needed for any use, which is why I am
writing rather than assuming. To be straight about what we are: the site is
free with no ads today, and there will be an app with a paid tier later, so I
would rather ask properly than call it non-commercial.

If there are trees you would rather we left off, say which and we will leave
them off. We already do that for trees whose owners want them private.

And whatever you decide about the data: if you know a live oak in New Orleans
that visitors should see and most lists miss, I would love to hear about it.

Hidde
ancienttrees.app

I would like to use the National Champion Trees register as a verification

an app with a paid tier is planned, so I would rather be clear now.

Would that be all right with you?

Hidde
ancienttrees.app

## 4. City of Sydney (Register of Significant Trees)

Rewritten 2026-08-16 in his own style, from a Dutch mail he supplied as the
calibration sample: name and what he builds in the first line, the goal stated
plainly (get people excited about old trees and outside looking at them), one
concrete ask, one personal question, done. The previous version was 330 words

To: Council@cityofsydney.nsw.gov.au (their own published open-data contact)
Subject: Licence for the Register of Significant Trees

---

Hello,

My name is Hidde and I am building ancienttrees.app, a platform for remarkable
trees. My goal is to get people excited about old trees and to encourage them to
go outside and look at them.

Your Register of Significant Trees is the best tree data I have found anywhere. I
would like to use it to build a Sydney page, credited to the City of Sydney with
a link back to the register.

Your street tree data carries a CC BY 4.0 licence, but the significant trees
layer has none, so I am asking rather than assuming. To be straight about what we
are: the site is free and carries no ads, but I am building an app with a paid
tier later, so I do not want to claim non-commercial. We would only publish trees
your own data marks as publicly accessible or visible from the street.

Would you be willing to licence it, CC BY or whatever you prefer?

And either way: which tree in Sydney do you think visitors should see?

Kind regards,
Hidde
ancienttrees.app
