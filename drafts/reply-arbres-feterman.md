# FOR HIDDE: reply to Georges Feterman, president of A.R.B.R.E.S.

**Who he is, because it changes what this is worth.** Their own site says the
association created the label "Arbre Remarquable de France" in the year 2000
and has awarded more than 800 of them, and Georges Feterman is its president.
So "votre choix est excellent" comes from the man whose association decides
which trees in France count as remarkable. That is the strongest thing anybody
has said to us.

He replied within hours of batch 006, from his private gmail rather than the
association address we wrote to, which is the fourth time that has happened
and the reason the inbox reader now matches on subject as well as sender.

**And the self-doubt is gone (Hidde, same day): "hij geeft al aan dat de bomen
goed zijn dus laat dat."** The question used to run "quel arbre nous manque,
et je suis a peu pres sur que le plus beau nous a echappe", which argues with
the compliment he just paid and fishes for reassurance. "Quel arbre
ajouteriez-vous" asks him for the same thing without implying we failed.

**The opening was too fawning and is now one line (Hidde, 2026-08-23).** It
read "venant du président de l'association qui a créé le label, cela me touche
vraiment", which flatters him by reciting his own title back at him. "Cela
compte venant de vous" makes the same point, that his opinion carries weight,
and lets him decide why.

**What I would ask, and what I would not.** He asked for nothing and gave a
compliment, so the reply should cost him ten seconds: which Paris tree do we
miss. That is flattering, it is the one thing only he can answer, and it is
the standing question at the end of every thread here.

What I would NOT ask yet is their label list. Those 800 labelled trees are the
best supply France has and they would open Lyon, Toulouse, Nice, Strasbourg
and Bordeaux at once, but asking a man for his association's database in the
same breath as thanking him for a compliment is the move that turns a warm
thread cold. It is the obvious second exchange, not this one. The mention of
the five other cities below is deliberately information rather than a request:
it tells him where the gaps are without asking him to fill them.

Checked before writing: Paris is live with 30 trees and returns 200; Lyon 13,
Toulouse 10, Nice 10, Strasbourg 10, Bordeaux 10.
