# FOR HIDDE: second reply to Hanna Hirsch, Bomenstichting Amsterdam

Two points in her mail of 2026-08-22, and both are real.

**1. The lorry never happened, and the source is her own organisation's.**
"Ook al staat het op de website van de Bomenstichting, de iep is niet
aangereden door een vrachtwagen." She is the Amsterdam branch of the body whose
national page carries that sentence, so this is not a source conflict to hedge:
the collision is out of the story entirely, not softened. What is left is what
anybody standing there can see, the low heavy branch and the prop under it.
The reason is written into the tree's verify_notes so no later pass can put the
lorry back from that same page.

**2. Ten trees behind a ticket, and she is right that it is too many.** Her
number was a third when she counted, and the honest answer is that it has been
moving in the right direction on its own: Amsterdam is 39 trees now, 29 of them
free, so the paid share is down from a third to a quarter, and every tree added
since her first mail has been a free one. The city register we already hold
carries 5,827 protected trees, so this is a matter of doing the work rather
than of finding permission.

What the mail does NOT do is promise a number or a date. Nothing about the
walk itself has changed yet, and saying otherwise to the person who caught two
errors already would be the worst possible move.

No third ask. She has now given us two corrections and a list of sources, and
her mail closes with "leuk als we het vervolg horen", which is an invitation to
report back rather than to request anything.
