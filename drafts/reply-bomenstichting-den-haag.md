# FOR HIDDE: reply to Clara Visser, chair of Bomenstichting Den Haag

The best answer batch 005 has produced. She gave four sources, and I checked
all four before writing so the reply says something true rather than thanks.

**What her links are actually worth:**

1. **The Haagse bomenapp is the real prize.** Its own metadata says 117,000
   street trees and nearly 1,300 monumental ones, and it names the city's open
   data platform plus `opendata@denhaag.nl` as the access contact. We publish
   FIVE trees in The Hague. This is the Den Bosch pattern again, a municipal
   ArcGIS layer, and it is the cheapest supply this project ever gets. What I
   could not settle today: the licence. The service path the app points at is a
   dead version (`V2_8...` returns 404) and the server hides its directory, so
   the honest next step is one mail to `opendata@denhaag.nl` asking which
   licence the monumental-tree layer carries. That is the municipality, not
   her, so it does not belong in this reply.
2. **hethaagsegroen.nl** carries their new monumental trees and a walking route
   called "Groen met historie", which is the same thing our city pages are.
3. **Their own site**, `haagsebomen.bomenstichtingdenhaag.nl`, is trees with
   stories across ten neighbourhoods, which is exactly our subject. It says
   "Copyright © 2025 | Bomenstichting Den Haag" and states no reuse licence, so
   we may verify facts against it and cite it, as we do with every other source,
   and we may not lift text or photographs. Asking her outright is worth doing
   later, in a thread that is warm; not in the first reply.
4. **monumentaltrees.com is deliberately not mentioned back.** Hard rule 1: we
   never use their content beyond fact verification, and the standing decision
   (2026-08-09) is not to write to them at all, because we are their direct
   competitor and a mail invites them to close the one zone their disclaimer
   leaves open. Saying "thanks, we will look there" to a third party would be
   the same mistake at one remove.

**The ask I chose, and why it is a tree and not a licence.** She is the chair of
a tree foundation who has just spent her lunch break sending a stranger four
links. The thing only she can give is which Hague tree belongs on the map, and
we are at five trees there, so the answer is immediately usable. The data
questions have their own addresses and can wait a week.
