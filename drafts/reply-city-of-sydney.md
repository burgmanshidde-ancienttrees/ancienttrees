# FOR HIDDE: reply to Karen Sweeney, Urban Forest Manager, City of Sydney

A clear no on licensing the Register, for a stated reason: they do not want the
data republished where it could later end up inside a paid product. Recorded in
OPEN_DATA_SURVEY.md so nobody scouts those 2,504 trees again.

**The mail does not argue.** She has thought about it and given a reason; the
one move that would cost us the relationship is a rebuttal. It accepts, states
one true fact, and asks one thing.

**The fact is worth stating because it answers her worry precisely.** Nothing on
our Sydney page comes from the Register. All six trees rest on the Botanic
Gardens, Museums of History NSW, Hunters Hill council, the BBC and Wikipedia,
each with its own sources. So the thing she is protecting is not on our site.

**And no promise about pricing**, deliberately. She raised paid products, and
the tempting reply is "the trees will always be free", which is exactly the
forever-promise that is Hidde's call and not mine, and worst of all in writing
to a third party. Saying what is published today is true and enough.

**The app is mentioned openly, on Hidde's instruction, and that is the right
call rather than a risk.** She has just told us she worries about data ending
up inside paid products. Announcing an app in the same breath could look like
confirming her fear, and the alternative, not mentioning it, would be worse:
she finds out later and the omission is what she remembers. So it is said
plainly and her opinion is asked, which is what one professional asks another.
Still no word about pricing.

**The ask is a tree, not the data.** Your instruction, and it is the right one
here: she cannot give us the Register, but she can name one tree, and that
costs her a sentence.
