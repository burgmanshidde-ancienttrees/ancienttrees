Dorit Zimmermann (BUND Naturschutz München), third mail in the thread, 2026-11-05. We sent her replies on 11-03 and 11-05 (send log). The tree ask (judge the Munich list) went out on 11-03 and she is doing it this weekend, so this reply asks for nothing new.

Her mail, in English, word for word:
"Dear Hidde, that makes sense and I am very happy to support you. Let's start with Munich. I write regularly for the Baumzeitung, a trade publication that is widely read in professional circles. I could imagine presenting your project as part of an article. That way it becomes better known. What do you think? You surely also work with the Champion Trees list, you'll find many old trees there too. There is also a book about special trees in NRW, you can pull a lot out of that too. At the weekend I'll look at Munich and then give you my feedback. I'll send you photos in good quality via Swisstransfer in exchange for name credit. Does that work for you? I'll send you the fan. Many greetings to NL, Dorit"

Facts checked: Munich has 63 trees, 36 with an approved photo. championtrees.de appears nowhere in our data, so the draft thanks her for the tip and makes no claim. Her photos go up with the licence "Used with permission from Dorit Zimmermann; credit required", which is what unique_photo() reads, so they also bring the Munich pages back into Google. No words of hers about a specific tree yet, so nothing for outreach-words.json.
