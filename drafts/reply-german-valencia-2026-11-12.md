Notes for Hidde

Germán de Huertas Villalba, head of the tree heritage section of València's parks service, answered our archive ask of today within twenty minutes. He points to the city geoportal: every tree catalogued as monumental under Ley 4/2006 has a ficha (record sheet) with photographs, downloadable from the attachments field; his two screenshots show the route. A ficha photo is the city's work, so we need his yes before one goes on a page (2026-11-10 rule: copyright needs the maker's yes). This reply asks only that.

His mail in English: "Good morning, through the geoportal tool of the València City Council you can get the record sheets of the trees catalogued as monumental under Law 4/2006 on the tree heritage of the city of València. These sheets include photographs of these trees. We attach photos of the route on the page and how to reach these sheets. In the attachments field, in the example with the name: if you click, the sheet for each tree downloads. Regards"
