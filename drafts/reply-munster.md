# FOR HIDDE: reply to Wolfgang Schürmann (Baumkunde.de, writes from Münster)

Your own text, in German. Same Wolfgang as the earlier thread; Münster is where
he lives, so the register behind him is Baumkunde.de.

I kept "Sie". You wrote "je", which is right in Dutch, but switching an older
German correspondent to "du" unprompted reads as presumptuous and he has used
"Sie" throughout. Nothing warm is lost by it.

You overruled my advice on the register question and your framing is better
than either option I gave you. I said not to ask, because he had offered links
and asking for his database in the same breath is a big jump. You ask instead
whether there is A German register whose data we may use. That puts nobody on
the spot, invites him to point at one, and leaves his own register as something
he can offer rather than something he has to defend.

What their imprint says, for when the answer comes: "Alle Texte und Bilder auf
Baumkunde.de unterliegen dem Urheberrecht, Verwendung nur nach ausdrücklicher
Genehmigung." So no open licence by default, and permission is the only route.
Facts are not copyrightable, but Germany has EU database rights, so
systematically lifting a register is not an option even where each fact is free.
