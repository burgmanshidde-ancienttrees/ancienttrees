# FOR HIDDE: reply to Giulia Torta, head of the Orto botanico di Firenze

**Your question: may we use their photographs? Not yet, and she is exactly the
person who can change that.** Nothing on their site carries an open licence, so
under hard rule 4 their images are off limits until somebody with authority
says otherwise in writing. She runs the garden, she wrote first, and she
pointed us at their own pages, so asking is natural rather than pushy.

**And the ask is small, which is why it will probably work.** Six of our seven
Orto botanico trees already have a photograph. Only the Himalayan cedar
(flo_014) has none. Asking for one photograph of one tree is a decision she can
take in a minute, and a yes there opens the door to the rest later.

**What is free either way**, and worth saying so we do not ask for what we
already have: the FACTS on their pages. Ages, measurements and history are not
copyrightable, so we may verify against their site and cite it, exactly as we
do with every register. Her confirmation that all seven sit in the Elenco degli
Alberi Monumentali d'Italia is itself the second source those entries wanted.

**Unusual Address is a lead, not something to ask her for.** She named an
association that runs tree walks with the garden and publishes a downloadable
map of every monumental tree in Florence. That is a contact for a later batch,
and it would be rude to ask her for their material.

Checked: Florence is live with 23 trees, seven of them in the Giardino dei
Semplici, and six of those seven carry a photograph.
