# FOR HIDDE: reply to Park Güell (Barcelona Serveis Municipals)

Two things in their mail, and the second one matters far more than the first.

**They said no to a link**, in plain words: they cannot advertise a private
initiative from their own site. That is a clean no and it is not worth arguing
with; a municipal company almost never can.

**And then they read our pages and found a real error.** Since 2020 the whole
of Park Güell needs a ticket and the free zone no longer exists. Four of our
Barcelona trees said the opposite, that the wooded area outside the Monumental
Zone was freely accessible. That is the access field, which after the pin is
the promise that decides whether somebody's afternoon works, and we had it
wrong on four trees for as long as they have been live.

Fixed before this reply: bcn_002, bcn_003, bcn_016 and bcn_033 now say a
ticket is required for the whole park, and each carries a verify_note quoting
their sentence so no later pass can restore the free-perimeter claim from an
older guide. Barcelona is 9 of 46 behind a ticket now, 19 percent, still well
inside the third.

**The one question worth asking back**, and it is precise enough for a desk to
answer in a line: they reviewed three trees, we have four with Park Güell
access. The fourth is the mastic at the rear entrance off Carrer del Torrent
del Remei, which our page describes as standing at a public access path rather
than inside the fence. That tree is flagged until they answer.

No second ask. They already said no to the link, and repeating it in the same
mail that thanks them for a correction would spend the goodwill their answer
just created.
