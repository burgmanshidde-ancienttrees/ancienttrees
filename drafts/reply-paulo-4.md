# FOR HIDDE: reply to Paulo Araujo, after he corrected us

He answered the mail of this morning with a correction and a joke. Both need a
short reply, and the correction needed work on the site first, which is done.

**He is right and we were wrong.** The mail asked him whether his Bischofia
"deserves a place", and it has had one since July: por_006, The Bischofia Trio
of the Botanical Garden, live with a story and a pin. He sent his photograph
BECAUSE it is on our Porto list, which he pointed out with a link to our own
page. The same is true of the second photograph I held back: the camellia walk
at Casa Tait is por_005, an ensemble entry that has been live since the city
opened.

**Why it happened, and what stops it happening again.** The eight photographs
were matched only against trees that had NO photograph, so the two of trees
that already had one came out the other end as trees we do not publish. That is
the same error passcheck.py and backlog.py were written for, one layer down:
assumed new, actually published. `check_leads_already_published()` in
scripts/preflight.py now refuses to let a lead somebody SENT us, or a lead whose
own words say we do not have this tree, sit next to a published tree of the same
genus in the same city. It matches on distance where there is a coordinate and
on genus plus a shared place phrase where there is not, never on the name alone.
It fires on both of his and on nothing else in 258 leads files.

**Both photographs are now on their trees**, credited to him like the other six:

- por_006, the Bischofia trio. Replaces an iNaturalist close-up of leaves and
  fruit, which is what the Cadiz standard rejects outright. His is the whole
  tree, in leaf, trunk and crown readable. Flat overcast light, and it still
  wins: the subject is the tree rather than a branch.
- por_005, the camellias of Casa Tait. Replaces a generic Camellia japonica
  bloom off Commons with no tie to Porto, which our own notes had been flagging
  as exactly that since 2026-07-29. His is the walk itself, in flower, with the
  house behind it.

Porto stays at 10 photographs and 8 of them are now his: both of these were
replacements rather than additions, which is the point. He did not fill two
holes, he beat two photographs that should not have been there.

**The smartphone.** He does not have one and is cheerful about it. Nothing to
fix and nothing to sell: the website has the same trees and works on any
computer, which is the whole answer. The app line in the last mail was mine to
get wrong, so the reply drops it rather than defending it.

**The tree question is Coimbra**, which is on the site with four trees and no
photograph at all. New ground rather than a second ask about Porto: he has
already said more Porto photographs are coming next week, and asking again for
those would read as need.

Not in the mail, but recorded: he says he knows no other Bischofia javanica in
mainland Portugal, and expects them in the islands. That agrees with what the
page already says from the council's classification, so it changes no sentence.
