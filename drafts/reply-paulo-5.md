# FOR HIDDE: reply to Paulo Araujo, his second batch of photographs

He sent four more by transfer on 2026-09-02 and said plainly he has no more of
Porto ("So tenho fotos de mais quatro"), so this reply does NOT ask him for
another Porto photograph. That would be asking for something he has just told
us he does not have.

**All four are live, and his filenames did the work again.** Aesculus, Fan-Palms,
Magnolias, Platanus, each with the place in the name. Three of the four I could
have matched from the picture alone; the magnolias I could not, because Porto has
TWO protected magnolia groups standing round a lake, Sao Lazaro and Arca d'Agua,
and his filename is the only thing that said which. Porto now carries a
photograph on 14 of 27 trees and 12 of them are his.

**One difference worth knowing, and it is why the mail mentions the file size.**
These arrived at 3008 pixels against roughly 1060 for his first batch. That is
the difference between a photograph we can show at the size it deserves and one
we have to keep small, and today it also exposed a fault of our own: our copy of
Wikimedia's list of available widths stopped at 960 and had been capping every
photograph on the site and in the app since July. Fixed the same day. None of
that belongs in the mail beyond the one line thanking him for the big files.

**No ask at all, and that is Hidde's correction (2026-09-03: "nee hij heeft
genoeg gegeven - antwoord paulo maar").** I had ended it with his Coimbra tip
turned outward, who else in Portugal is worth writing to, on the standing rule
that every thread ends with a question. He cut it. Paulo has now sent twelve
photographs and a working lead in two weeks, and a thank-you that arrives
carrying another request is not really a thank-you.

So the rule has a condition it did not have this morning: end with a question
when the thread is still finding out what somebody has, and stop asking when
they have already given. The mail still says the Coimbra tip is being used, and
that is not an ask, it is the best thanks available: his suggestion produced a
letter. `drafts/coimbra-jardim-botanico.md` is that letter, ready to go
alongside this one.
