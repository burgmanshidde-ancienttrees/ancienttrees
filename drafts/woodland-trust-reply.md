Hi Kate,

Thanks for coming back to me so quickly. www.ancienttrees.app is live now as a
website, go and have a look! I am working on an iOS and Android app at the
moment while gathering more data.

Ancienttrees.app maps remarkable ancient trees city by city, tells the story of
each one, and strings a few into a short walk you can do in an afternoon. There
are 95 cities and 893 trees live right now, 8 of those cities in the UK.

I am building this as a private individual, with no institution behind it and
no outside funding. The site combines official registers, automated checking
and input from readers. It is free to use today, and a paid tier is possible
later for things like routes and offline maps.

I would not be publishing a copy of the Inventory. I would make a selection of
trees per city and turn those into walkable clusters.

Keeping that current is largely automated on my side, so it is far less of a
burden than it would once have been. Trees do die. It works two ways: the site
can check in with your data set regularly and take your lead on it, and readers
correct what they find on the ground.

Thanks for taking it to your colleagues.
