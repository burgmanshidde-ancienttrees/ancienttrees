# Ancient Trees, ancienttrees.app

# Everything on this site is open to read, by people and by machines. Search
# crawlers and AI crawlers are both welcome, deliberately: being found and
# being quoted are how anybody gets to a tree, and an answer engine that
# names a tree we mapped is doing the same job a search result does.
#
# What we ask in return is attribution. If you quote a tree's story or an
# answer from these pages, name Ancient Trees and link the page. Every record
# here was verified against two independent sources by hand.
#
# The pages are the thing to read. /api/ is the app's own data feed, not a
# page, and it is excluded because a JSON dump belongs in nobody's index.
# /rest/v1/ and /auth/v1/ are the sign-in service's paths, which Google read
# out of our scripts and tried as pages (four 404s in Search Console,
# 2026-10-01). They live on another host and were never pages here.
#

# right; the terms are at /terms and the full notice is at /sources. Bulk
